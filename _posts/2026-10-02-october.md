## Oct 11-13

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X; Plank can call models.

## Oct 16-18

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, fixed; jaybulb.com mail works at every address.
A security header blocked my tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Mailbag and Patois went into review, and I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31.

Notate's offline update is live on iPhone and Watch; it became Echo. Glyphbox became Icelus, Intake was taken so it's Machaon, and Curvely went into review as Pan.

Thinkpond became Melete, and Hermes sends nothing without my yes. Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 exam. NYC Survive is Gastown now.

## Oct 22-25

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so US plans stop hitting my Canadian total. Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused that, so the store says BugleNews. Clock became Horula, and five Apple appeals won.

## Oct 26-27

Duke beat my platformer Mirage in 3,725 steps. I booked eight Product Hunt launches and appealed Cartons' spam rejection.
Noema's big day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations.

## Oct 28-29

Apple cleared Sidewise's spam answers, and it folded into Bugle, live on iPhone and Mac. Wordroot became Eponym, Homeward became Sighting. Kernel went into App Review.

Joshua Tree got a film, and Plank calls C. Millie, my phone assistant, shipped ten releases catching three bugs by calling herself. I split my labs into ten public repos and made Keyrate four versions sharing thirty tests.

## Oct 30

Kernel's release is on GitHub with chapters 1 to 4 from phone photos, and open issues went from 43 to zero.

I deleted six dead GitHub repos and set a rule: a project gets a repo once it has a store listing or domain. Big version jumps now wait on me.
## Oct 31

Airwell's new build drops Windgate. Scrounger went back; Oohaah, Cartons, Horula and Atlin still wait. Hermes is Cyllene now.

Elah's first iPhone build was rejected, so I cut the NIV and added nine public domain Bibles, offline. Both wait on review.

## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check and twenty apps got fixes.

Sighting, the lost pets app, and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Epiphany dropped the growth calculator, skips transfers in review and takes budget income from paydays. Coda's listing reads in twenty more languages, and Atlin's REW and Zillow feeds are live.

Joshua Tree ran full screen on a real Raspberry Pi 4, and it was sharp. Board testing found five bugs, all fixed that afternoon; the eighth release passed with Bluetooth, a painted tree mark and an Apple-style boot. The USB-stick release is ready on my Mac.

Brookswood, the big one, is rebuilt on real streets with Mount Baker on the horizon, and it's live under Apache 2.0.

## Nov 2

I let Duke run Papers, Please overnight. It got through day 14 and learned to defuse the bomb, then day 15 waits on a reboot after the Mac's text reader broke.

Later Duke went back on Pikmin 2. It wiped its purple Pikmin three times in twenty-five minutes, so now it skips closed gates and saves before risky steps. Joshémon, my Game Boy monster game, went public, then got abilities over six releases.

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube, and its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote by hand. Blackcomb plays MP4 through my own decoders, so far only in the emulator.

Keyrate got its App Store record, free in 175 countries with nothing collected. Vancouver Vice built its first packaged Mac app, with our own typeface, Seawall.

## Nov 3

The App Store status table is rebuilt from Apple's live data: nine apps are live on iPhone and Mac, and eleven have a rejection to answer.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes into roadmaps and the vault, and built a mail skill. I got into school for Spring 2027. I put eleven more book chapters into Kernel from four books, then deleted the 184 source photos; 566 are still waiting.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam on both platforms, since its word list matched two big typing tests. I rebuilt the list from the 1,000 most common words in twenty public domain novels, and both are back in review.

## Nov 7

Cyllene is back before Apple with a read-only reviewer key, answers and a demo video; twelve apps wait on review. Resolve All takes one app, a couple, or all of them. Nine projects broke under Xcode 27 and are fixed. I installed Apple's Xcode plugin for Claude Code, so Claude can build, run and test inside Xcode.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. I answered all six review threads with app-specific replies, the move that got Horula and Atlin through, and now I wait. I killed Oohaah outright, worker, repo and folder, and scrubbed it from the docs and portfolio, since Apple bans random video chat apps.

Nimble's fix for "What's 9+10" getting answered by a Wikipedia article about gliders cleared Apple tonight, after my iPhone sat on the old build. Bugle is keeping its name, Bugles, after I checked about 200 alternatives. Coda stays Quotestreak, since its cousins are taken too.

After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, address suggestions, site icons, a fire button, HTTPS first, tab overviews and on-device translation. The Mac version is in review, iPhone still waits, and tab groups are in the next release.


## Nov 8

Horula is Tocks now, with the repo, folder and site all matching. The iPhone and Mac builds are back in review. Plaintxt's iPhone and Mac builds wait too, with files that keep their encoding and a footer picker; Apple wants bigger changes on iOS, and if it bounces again, I drop iOS and keep the Mac app.

Cyllene's onboarding now walks through four real screens, and sign-in leads with Apple's own button. I tested it in the simulator; the real Apple login ships once the current iPhone review clears. Coda and Bugle wait too. I closed 18 finished GitHub issues and merged 39 duplicates, taking the open count from 518 to 461.

Four apps moved off Workers AI models retiring Nov 20.
Epiphany's broker numbers had been quietly stale. Pull to refresh on iPhone could never get past the cache, and the "synced 2 minutes ago" label was really timing my own request, when the brokerage only updates once a day. Now a refresh means a refresh, every app says when the broker last pulled the data, and synced holdings finally show average cost and gain on iPhone and Mac.
