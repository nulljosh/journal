Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so US plans stop hitting my Canadian total. Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused, so the store says BugleNews. Five Apple appeals won.

## Oct 26-27

Duke beat my platformer Mirage in 3,725 steps. Noema's big day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations.

## Oct 28-29

Apple cleared Sidewise's spam answers; it folded into Bugle, live on iPhone and Mac. Homeward became Sighting. Kernel went into App Review.

Joshua Tree got a film, and Plank calls C. Millie, my phone assistant, shipped ten releases catching three bugs by calling herself. I split my labs into ten public repos and made Keyrate four versions sharing thirty tests.

## Oct 30

Kernel's release is on GitHub with chapters 1 to 4 from phone photos; open issues went from 43 to zero.

I deleted six dead GitHub repos and set a rule: a repo needs a store listing or domain.

## Oct 31

Airwell's new build drops Windgate. Scrounger went back; Cartons, Horula and Atlin still wait.
Elah's first iPhone build was rejected, so I cut the NIV and added nine public domain Bibles, offline; both wait.

## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check; twenty apps got fixes.

Sighting and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Epiphany dropped the growth calculator, skips transfers in review and takes budget income from paydays.
Joshua Tree ran full screen on a real Raspberry Pi 4, sharp. Board testing found five bugs, fixed that afternoon; release eight passed with Bluetooth.

## Nov 2

I let Duke run Papers, Please overnight; it got through day 14 and learned to defuse the bomb.

Joshemon, my Game Boy game, went public, then got six ability releases.

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube, and its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote by hand.
Keyrate got its App Store record, free in 175 countries and collecting nothing. Vancouver Vice built its first Mac app, with our own typeface, Seawall.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes into roadmaps and the vault, and built a mail skill. I added eleven Kernel chapters from four books and deleted 184 source photos; 566 still wait.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam on both platforms, matching its word list to two typing tests. I rebuilt its word list from public domain novels, and both are back in review.

## Nov 7

Cyllene is back before Apple with a reviewer key and a demo video; twelve apps wait. Nine projects broke under Xcode 27, all fixed. I installed Apple's Xcode plugin so Claude can build and test.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. I answered all six review threads with app-specific replies; that got Horula and Atlin through.
Bugle is keeping its name, Bugles, after checking 200 others.

After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, site icons, a fire button, HTTPS first and translation. Mac is in review, iPhone waits.

## Nov 8

iPhone and Mac builds are back in review. Plaintxt's builds wait; Apple wants bigger iOS changes or I drop iOS.

Cyllene's onboarding has four real screens; real login ships after iPhone review.
Epiphany's broker numbers were stale; pull to refresh hit the cache.

## Nov 9

The iPhone and Mac update is in review; the web is live. Synced holdings show how old the broker's data is, and cost basis and gains reach the apps.

Joshua Tree's next build merged with 32 issues closed, including Focus mode and Pi ping; PC ping is still open.

Epiphany's iPhone statement upload never worked, so my October statement missed the graph. The server read the same PDF fine, so the bug was the phone app. The fix reads iCloud files and shows errors in red, untested on a phone.

jaybulb.com email is live through iCloud+; only info@ receives so far.

The journal borrowed colours from an old stylesheet, so some dividers vanished; it has its own now.

## Nov 10

Hamurapi's dark landing shows the game at night, and I cut a trailer, and it is approved on both platforms; price and store link are still to do.

Millie stopped treating me like a stranger and tells me what came in each week.

Apple rejected Patois for not signing in with Apple, which was never spam. The sheet stayed open after sign-in, so it looked dead. Fixed; the iPhone build is back in review.

## Nov 11

Typed math answers count in any order, and the math courses show x², √2 and π. Pre-Calc 11 went from ten to sixty questions; Pre-Calc 12 explains every answer. iOS waits so the sign-in review stays put.

I built a Things page for jaybulb.com: 55 things I own and use, 23 with covers. I also wrote an onboarding skill and a trailer skill. Airwell's landing now has one breathing circle; Cyllene's has one app list.

Patois still said Tonchi in password reset emails, so I fixed that and swept out the old names. The web is live; the sign-in fix is with Apple.

## Nov 13

Apple locked my developer account until I accepted a new agreement. Tocks was rejected for background audio, but sleep sounds play on a locked phone, so I asked for a bug fix. Apple asked what Delete account does, and the server now removes lost-pet posts. Pan became Conics after a rejection, but Apple still says the version isn't ready. Co-Stanza is live on iPhone and Mac.

## Nov 14

Sent an iPhone update to Apple. I tried a one-word Nimble, but the bare name belongs to another developer.

I spent the evening on Joshua Tree's Energy Saver: the screen dims, a clock drifts, then the HDMI goes dark. It sat on its own branch with no pull request until today.

## Nov 15

I spent the morning making Nimble faster, and most questions come back in under a second. Settled facts stay cached three days, so repeats feel instant.

Joshua Tree's screen dimming and screen saver are merged, and I re-cut its landing trailer in the Fable style. The Pi's auto lock is in review: the saver or Sleep locks the screen, and only my password opens it. Printing to a network printer is written too, and the goals after 13 are goals, not version numbers.
