## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree's second release sandboxed twenty-six apps; Bookrank works offline on iPhone. Auto-trading is live.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review. Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5 to 8

Apple rejected seven apps as spam, and Hikko over screenshots.

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1080p, and joined Wi-Fi once I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked. I wrote Joshua Tree's book and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11 to 13

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X; Plank can call models. Joshua Tree's landing is live.

## Oct 16 to 18

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, now fixed; jaybulb.com mail works at every address. All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Mailbag and Patois went into review, and stocks came back.

I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history.

## Oct 19 to 21

Notate's offline update is live on iPhone and Watch. Glyphbox became Icelus; its App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now, Thinkpond became Melete, and Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect, sends nothing without my yes. I made my portfolio one ranked list.

Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam; wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon and 45 sweep problems closed. The calling app became Oohaah after three names, both in review. Notate became Echo.

Patois uses its new name and its streak-freeze crash is fixed; Temida's Apple sign-in fix cleared. Intake was taken, so it's Machaon. Curvely's second release went into review as Pan.

NYC Survive is Gastown now: a crew, a Steam Clock and self-running drills, no raids or cash. A headless bot proved it winnable in twelve days. I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site without made-up prices, and Doorstock went to Apple.

## Oct 22 to 25

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so US plans stop hitting my Canadian total.

Joshua Tree's core shrank by half, and Samantha answers on the Pi without my Mac.

Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused plain Bugle; the store says BugleNews. Clock became Horula. Five Apple appeals won; Atlin's store name is back.

## Oct 26 to 27

I made Mirage, a desert platformer Duke beat in 3,725 steps, and Turkish grew from 11 units to 120 on Patois. I booked eight Product Hunt launches for Oct 28 to Nov 6 and appealed Cartons' spam rejection.

Kaggle suspended my account; training waits on appeal. Curbfind became Scrounger, since Apple refused plain Scrounge.

Noema's biggest day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and C matched it. Maybe an OEIS term for a 2020 Aaronson question. In medicine, ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations and 61 confidence intervals in 52 trials with a lost minus sign.

## Oct 28 to 29

Apple cleared my Sidewise spam answers, so I pulled it into Bugle. Bugle's iPhone and Mac builds are live. Epiphany's news thumbnails shipped; MarketWatch went from 25 to 84 stories.

Wordroot became Eponym. Homeward became Sighting. Kernel went into App Review, then added a sleep timer, three narration languages, Goodreads sign-in and AI-free quizzes.

Placid Platypus is known through 181; a search cracked 177, 178 and 188. I opened the AI lane: 169 of 182 SWE-bench Verified submissions can't be checked. I machine-checked the triangular Ising proof's weakest step. Duke cleared Emergence Cave on Pikmin 2.

Joshua Tree got a launch film and a teaching page, Plank calls C, and the Pi gets tested this weekend. Millie, my phone assistant, shipped ten releases with spam blocking and caller memory, and caught three bugs by calling her own number.

I split my labs into ten public repos; GitHub's limit holds four more back. I turned Keyrate from one web page into four versions sharing thirty tests.

## Oct 30

Epiphany's web, iPhone and Mac cards are in review.

Kernel's release is on GitHub, with chapters 1 to 4 of Video Game Design For Dummies from phone photos. Open issues went from 43 to zero. A coverless book now shows its title.

The map has rain radar and storm tracks; stock pages show insider trades. Millie got a launch film.

Atlin's sample homes stay on land, and Temida stops panning on iPhone.

Bugle stops showing one paper's copy once I follow another, and Hacker News links work. I fixed the Noema links the labs split broke; Melete's posts show each idea's spec and build plan.

I renamed the Bible app Elah, since Apple had the plain name; it gained highlights, notes, search and listening.

I deleted six dead GitHub repos, moved scratch folders to the LaCie, and wrote a rule: a project gets a repo once it has a store listing or domain. I sent 34 Apple Notes into 29 roadmaps.

I picked eight of eighteen unlaunched apps for Product Hunt, weekly from Nov 13.

I gave Joshua Tree's Dock a Magnify setting. I tightened the rules: big jumps wait on me, features are minors, fixes are patches, shipped numbers never change.

Tripwire covers every repo, but it burned most of an AI credit and misses most of what's left. Shipped by hand: Temida's money bars stopped animating and every headline uses one font; Epiphany's donut converts US dollars before adding cash; Atlin's browse page fits a phone; and Airwell went public.

## Oct 31

Millie quotes my status and texts back as herself, and booked a family movie for Sunday. Elah got reading plans on Today. I summarized chapter two of Hackers and Painters and fixed the iPad button.

I filed thirteen Apple Notes into roadmaps and the wiki, then emptied Notes. I trashed fourteen stale wiki backups. Atlin's landing got a faded photo behind the hero, and its headline opens listings. Epiphany's charts got five-year and all-time ranges, and crypto prices stream about once a second.

Every open rejection got an answer or a new build. Patois now signs in through my own server, fixing Apple's Sign in with Apple error. Mailbag's Mac window stopped resizing itself in a loop at sign-in. Airwell's new build says Airwell, not the old Windgate build Apple reviewed. Scrounger went back with newer features, Blackcomb answered Apple's nine questions, and Oohaah, Cartons, Horula and Atlin wait on Apple.

Hermes is Cyllene now, bundle id aside. Its iPhone app got App Lock and a TestFlight tab, and it translates What's New into every store language on the device, saving them all after one yes. The last two wait for the current version's review.

Elah's first iPhone build was rejected, and Apple sign-in is throttled, so I haven't read why. I cut the NIV and put nine public domain Bibles in as real text, offline, with a picker in the app and on the web. The headline is now Read the Bible, anywhere. Both are waiting for review.
