## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree's second release sandboxed twenty-six apps; Bookrank works offline on iPhone. Auto-trading is live.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review. Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses.

## Oct 5 to 8

Apple rejected seven apps as spam, and Hikko over screenshots.

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1080p once I wrote the Wi-Fi login its chip lacked.

## Oct 11 to 13

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X; Plank can call models. Joshua Tree has a landing.

## Oct 16 to 18

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, now fixed; jaybulb.com mail works at every address. All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman, and I fixed ten repos. Mailbag and Patois went into review, and I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history.

## Oct 19 to 21

Notate's offline update is live on iPhone and Watch. Glyphbox became Icelus, and its App Store build waits.

Temida is my litigation app now, Thinkpond became Melete, and Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect, sends nothing without my yes. Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam, and wrong word problems fell from 46 to 2.

The calling app became Oohaah after three names, both in review. Notate became Echo, Temida's Apple sign-in fix cleared, Intake was taken so it's Machaon, and Curvely went into review as Pan.

NYC Survive is Gastown now: a crew, a Steam Clock and self-running drills, no raids or cash. I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site without made-up prices, and Doorstock went to Apple.

## Oct 22 to 25

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so US plans stop hitting my Canadian total. Joshua Tree's core shrank by half, and Samantha answers on the Pi without my Mac.

Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused that, so the store says BugleNews. Clock became Horula, and five Apple appeals won.

## Oct 26 to 27

Duke beat my desert platformer Mirage in 3,725 steps, and I booked eight Product Hunt launches for Oct 28 to Nov 6 and appealed Cartons' spam rejection. Kaggle suspended my account, so training waits on appeal. Curbfind became Scrounger, since Apple refused Scrounge.

Noema's biggest day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and C matched it; ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations and 61 confidence intervals in 52 trials with a lost minus sign.

## Oct 28 to 29

Apple cleared my Sidewise spam answers, so I pulled it into Bugle, whose iPhone and Mac builds are live. Wordroot became Eponym, and Homeward became Sighting. Kernel went into App Review, then added a sleep timer, three narration languages, Goodreads sign-in and AI-free quizzes.

Placid Platypus is known through 181, and 169 of 182 SWE-bench Verified submissions can't be checked.

Joshua Tree got a launch film and Plank calls C. Millie, my phone assistant, shipped ten releases with spam blocking and caller memory, catching three bugs by calling her own number.

I split my labs into ten public repos, with GitHub's limit holding four more back, and turned Keyrate into four versions sharing thirty tests.

## Oct 30

Kernel's release is on GitHub, with chapters 1 to 4 from phone photos; open issues went from 43 to zero.

I fixed the Noema links the labs split broke.

I renamed the Bible app Elah, since Apple had the plain name. I deleted six dead GitHub repos and set a rule: a project gets a repo once it has a store listing or domain. I sent 34 Apple Notes into 29 roadmaps, and picked eight of eighteen unlaunched apps for Product Hunt, weekly from Nov 13.

I tightened the rules: big jumps wait on me, features are minors, fixes are patches, shipped numbers never change.

Tripwire covers every repo, but it burned most of an AI credit and misses most of what's left. Shipped by hand: Temida's money bars stopped animating and every headline uses one font; Airwell went public.

## Oct 31

Millie quotes my status and texts back, Elah got reading plans on Today, and I fixed the iPad button.

I filed thirteen Apple Notes into roadmaps and the wiki, then emptied Notes. Epiphany's charts got five-year and all-time ranges, and crypto prices stream about once a second.

Every open rejection got an answer or new build. Patois signs in through my server now, which fixes Apple's Sign in with Apple error. Airwell's new build says Airwell, not Windgate. Scrounger went back, Blackcomb answered Apple's nine questions, and Oohaah, Cartons, Horula and Atlin still wait.

Hermes is Cyllene now, bundle id aside. Its iPhone app got App Lock, a TestFlight tab, a What's New translator, sales at a glance, a share card and a launch film, all waiting for review.

Elah's first iPhone build was rejected, and Apple sign-in is throttled. I cut the NIV and put nine public domain Bibles in as real text, offline, with a picker in app and web. The headline is now Read the Bible, anywhere; both wait on review.

## Nov 1

Spent the day on accessibility. An automated check of all 60 landing and 38 web app pages is now clean; about twenty apps got fixes. The shared jaybulb.com grey hit 4.9 to 1, so faint text passes everywhere, and I fixed its dead links.

Keyrate got desktop, iPad and Homebrew apps and a Test menu. Co-Stanza lets you edit a poem in place, take kudos only the author counts, follow poets and read a Yours feed online. Noema's proof check kept running out of memory on CI, so big proofs build one at a time.

Epiphany dropped the growth calculator, swipes its ticker over Fear and Greed, and skips transfers in review. Scrounger's deal search stopped timing out, Nimble's mascot became a lightbulb, and hero headlines are taglines, not app names.

I got a 27 inch monitor and ran Joshua Tree full screen on the real Raspberry Pi 4 for the first time, and it came up sharp with Wi-Fi up from the internet. The first board pass found five bugs, all fixed that afternoon, from a screen that slept with no keyboard to a big web page that knocked the network out. Wi-Fi, Settings, the debugger and on-Pi building all worked. Releases now carry the Pi kernel too, the first step toward the Pi updating itself.

The landings changed most. Nearly all now run the real product behind the hero, with parallax, checked in light, dark and on a phone: Nimble answers its own questions, Epiphany's live map sits under its copy, Patois lessons answer themselves, Kernel reads chapters aloud, Plank compiles real programs, Duke plays real game frames and Turing's Samantha answers her FAQ. Bugle streams headlines, Scrounger and Sighting show listings, and Echo writes its transcripts. Eight landings dropped custom fonts for the bold system font, Oohaah went jungle green, nine footers end on the app's own glyphs, and Coda and plaintxt stop scrolling sideways on phones.
