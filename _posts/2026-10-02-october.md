Duke beat my platformer Mirage in 3,725 steps. Noema's big day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations.

## Oct 30

Airwell's new build drops Windgate. Scrounger went back; Cartons, Horula and Atlin still wait.

## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check; twenty apps got fixes.

## Nov 2

I let Duke run Papers, Please overnight; it reached day 14 and learned to defuse the bomb.

Joshemon, my Game Boy game, went public, then got six releases.

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube; its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote by hand.
Keyrate got its App Store record, free in 175 countries and collecting nothing. Vancouver Vice built its first Mac app, with our own typeface, Seawall.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes into roadmaps and the vault. I added eleven Kernel chapters from four books and deleted 184 source photos; 566 still wait.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam, matching its word list to two typing tests. I rebuilt the list from public domain novels; both are back in review.

## Nov 7

Cyllene is back before Apple with a reviewer key and demo video; twelve apps wait. Nine projects broke under Xcode 27; all are fixed.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. App-specific replies to all six threads got Horula and Atlin through.

After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, site icons, a fire button, HTTPS first and translation. Mac is in review, iPhone waits.

## Nov 8

iPhone and Mac builds are back in review. Plaintxt's builds wait; Apple wants bigger iOS changes or I drop iOS.

Cyllene's onboarding has four real screens; real login ships after iPhone review.

## Nov 9

The iPhone and Mac update is in review; the web is live. Synced holdings show how old the broker's data is; cost basis and gains reach the apps.

Joshua Tree's next build merged, closing 32 issues, including Focus mode and Pi ping; PC ping is open.

Epiphany's iPhone statement upload never worked, so my October statement missed the graph; the fix is untested.

## Nov 10

Hamurapi's dark landing shows the game at night; its trailer is approved on both platforms, price and store link to do.

Apple rejected Patois for not signing in with Apple, which was never spam. The sheet stayed open after sign-in, so it looked dead. Fixed; the iPhone build is back in review.

## Nov 11

Typed math answers count in any order, and math courses show x², √2 and π. Pre-Calc 11 went from ten to sixty questions; Pre-Calc 12 explains every answer. iOS waits so the sign-in review stays put.

I built a Things page for jaybulb.com: 55 things I use, 23 with covers. 
Patois still said Tonchi in password reset emails; I fixed that and swept out old names. The sign-in fix is with Apple.

## Nov 13

Apple locked my developer account until I accepted a new agreement. Tocks was rejected for background audio, but sleep sounds play on a locked phone, so I asked for a fix. Apple asked what Delete account does; the server now removes lost-pet posts. Pan became Conics after rejection; Apple says it isn't ready. Co-Stanza is live on iPhone and Mac.

## Nov 14

Sent an iPhone update to Apple. The one-word Nimble name belongs to another developer.

Joshua Tree's Energy Saver sat on a branch until today; I spent the evening on it.

## Nov 15

I spent the morning making Nimble faster; most answers take under a second.

Joshua Tree's dimming and saver are merged, and its landing trailer is re-cut. The Pi's auto lock is in review: the saver or Sleep locks it, and only my password opens it. Network printing is written too.

I checked Doorstock, the garage door parts app, against its spec and built the three missing pieces: spring specs with costs, a label scanner, and a monthly accountant CSV. Mac is built, unsubmitted; iPhone waits on the spam appeal. I also prototyped Garage Cam, a shelf camera that asks if I took a part or added one.

Echo 3.1 added Word export everywhere and moved its Mac hot key to Control-Shift-Space, since macOS already uses Control-Option-Space for input sources.

Talli got a money log for earnings and expenses, with a Home bar for my yearly earnings limit that feeds the tax-time export. The web and server are live; iPhone and Mac still need TestFlight.

Summarizing Effective C from a PDF was quicker. Then my Kernel library came up empty: its summaries went missing after November 6, and I don't know why yet. Getting them back is next.

Epiphany is free on the web now; Pro is $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year after a 7-day trial, and anyone who paid $1 before keeps it for life. I also shut three billing and TradingView signal holes, testing each live. The iPhone paywall is next. I also cold-emailed two local plumbers about web design, with the unsubscribe line Canadian law requires. Checking their sites caught a wrong pitch: one draft died, the other went out.
