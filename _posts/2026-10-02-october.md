## Oct 1-3

Joshua Tree's second release sandboxed twenty-six apps; Bookrank works offline on iPhone.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.
## Oct 5-8

Apple rejected seven apps as spam, Hikko over screenshots.

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1080p once I wrote its Wi-Fi login.

## Oct 11-13

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X; Plank can call models.

## Oct 16-18

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, fixed; jaybulb.com mail works at info@.
A security header blocked my tokens file and pages fell back to Times New Roman, so I fixed ten repos. Mailbag and Patois went into review, and I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31.

## Oct 19-21

Notate's offline update is live on iPhone and Watch; it became Echo. Glyphbox became Icelus, and Curvely went into review as Pan.

Thinkpond became Melete, and Hermes sends nothing without my yes. Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 exam.
## Oct 22-25

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so US plans stop hitting my Canadian total. Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused, so the store says BugleNews. Five Apple appeals won.

## Oct 26-27

Duke beat my platformer Mirage in 3,725 steps. I booked eight Product Hunt launches and appealed Cartons' spam rejection.
Noema's big day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations.

## Oct 28-29

Apple cleared Sidewise's spam answers, and it folded into Bugle, live on iPhone and Mac. Homeward became Sighting. Kernel went into App Review.

Joshua Tree got a film, and Plank calls C. Millie, my phone assistant, shipped ten releases catching three bugs by calling herself. I split my labs into ten public repos and made Keyrate four versions sharing thirty tests.

## Oct 30

Kernel's release is on GitHub with chapters 1 to 4 from phone photos, and open issues went from 43 to zero.

I deleted six dead GitHub repos and set a rule: a project gets one once it has a store listing or domain.

## Oct 31

Airwell's new build drops Windgate. Scrounger went back; Cartons, Horula and Atlin still wait.
Elah's first iPhone build was rejected, so I cut the NIV and added nine public domain Bibles, offline. Both wait on review.

## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check, and twenty apps got fixes.

Sighting and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Epiphany dropped the growth calculator, skips transfers in review and takes budget income from paydays. Coda's listing is in twenty more languages.

Joshua Tree ran full screen on a real Raspberry Pi 4, sharp. Board testing found five bugs, fixed that afternoon; the eighth release passed with Bluetooth, a painted tree mark and an Apple-style boot.

Brookswood, the big one, is rebuilt on real streets with Mount Baker on the horizon, under Apache 2.0.

## Nov 2

I let Duke run Papers, Please overnight. It got through day 14 and learned to defuse the bomb; day 15 waits on a reboot after the Mac's text reader broke.

Later Duke went back to Pikmin 2 and wiped its purple Pikmin three times in twenty-five minutes, so it now skips closed gates and saves first. Joshemon, my Game Boy game, went public, then got six ability releases.

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube, and its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote by hand. Blackcomb plays MP4 through my own decoders, so far only in the emulator.

Keyrate got its App Store record, free in 175 countries with nothing collected. Vancouver Vice built its first packaged Mac app, with our own typeface, Seawall.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes into roadmaps and the vault, and built a mail skill. I put eleven more book chapters into Kernel from four books and deleted 184 source photos; 566 still wait.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam on both platforms; its word list matched two big typing tests. I rebuilt it from the 1,000 most common words in twenty public domain novels; both are back in review.

## Nov 7

Cyllene is back before Apple with a read-only reviewer key and a demo video; twelve apps wait. Nine projects broke under Xcode 27 and are fixed. I installed Apple's Xcode plugin, so Claude can build, run and test inside Xcode.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. I answered all six review threads with app-specific replies, the move that got Horula and Atlin through. I killed Oohaah outright, since Apple bans random video chat apps.

Nimble's fix for a glider article answering "What's 9+10" cleared Apple tonight. Bugle is keeping its name, Bugles, after I checked about 200 others.

After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, address suggestions, site icons, a fire button, HTTPS first, tab overviews and on-device translation. Mac is in review, iPhone waits.

## Nov 8

The iPhone and Mac builds are back in review. Plaintxt's builds wait too; Apple wants bigger changes on iOS, and if it bounces again I drop iOS and keep the Mac app.

Cyllene's onboarding now has four real screens and leads sign-in with Apple's own button. I tested it in the simulator; real Apple login ships after the iPhone review. Coda and Bugle wait too. I closed 18 finished GitHub issues and merged 39 duplicates.

Epiphany's broker numbers had been quietly stale: pull to refresh never got past the cache, and the "synced 2 minutes ago" label timed my own request, not the broker, which updates daily.

## Nov 9

The iPhone and Mac update is with Apple for review, and the web is live. A refresh now means a refresh, synced holdings show how old the broker's data is, and cost basis and gains reach the apps through the server. Why holdings show as days old is still open.

Joshua Tree's next build is merged, with 32 issues closed. It adds PC window snapping, QEMU debugging on both ports, man pages for Terminal commands, new apps on reused disks, Focus mode and Pi ping. I hit the session limit before PC ping, still open.

Epiphany's iPhone statement upload had never worked reliably, so my October statement missed the spending graph. The server took the same PDF when I sent it directly, so the bug is in the phone app. The fix reads iCloud files properly and shows errors as a red row. I haven't tried it on a real phone yet.

jaybulb.com email is live through iCloud+. info@jaybulb.com is verified, and a test sent from it came back. My dad got the first email from the new address. Only info@ receives so far.

The journal borrowed colours from an old portfolio stylesheet, and four colour names it used never existed, so a divider and some grey labels were quietly missing. It has its own colour file now, with a blue accent and the system font, and the old fonts and 24 unused header pictures are gone. A QA pass fixed a doubled heading, uneven cards, overlapping year-chart labels and a theme button sitting on words on phones.
