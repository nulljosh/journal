## Oct 1-3

Joshua Tree's second release sandboxed twenty-six apps; Bookrank works offline on iPhone. 

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review. Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses.

## Oct 5-8

Apple rejected seven apps as spam, Hikko over screenshots.

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1080p once I wrote the Wi-Fi login its chip lacked.

## Oct 11-13

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X; Plank can call models. Joshua Tree has a landing.

## Oct 16-18

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, fixed; jaybulb.com mail works at every address. All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Mailbag and Patois went into review, and I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31.

## Oct 19-21

Notate's offline update is live on iPhone and Watch; it became Echo. Glyphbox became Icelus, Temida's Apple sign-in fix cleared, Intake was taken so it's Machaon, and Curvely went into review as Pan.

Thinkpond became Melete, and Hermes sends nothing without my yes. Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 exam, and wrong word problems fell from 46 to 2. NYC Survive is Gastown now: a crew, a Steam Clock, no raids or cash.

## Oct 22-25

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so US plans stop hitting my Canadian total. Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused that, so the store says BugleNews. Clock became Horula, and five Apple appeals won.

## Oct 26-27

Duke beat my platformer Mirage in 3,725 steps. I booked eight Product Hunt launches for Oct 28 to Nov 6 and appealed Cartons' spam rejection. Curbfind became Scrounger.

Noema's big day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations.

## Oct 28-29

Apple cleared my Sidewise spam answers, so I folded it into Bugle, live on iPhone and Mac. Wordroot became Eponym, Homeward became Sighting. Kernel went into App Review.

Joshua Tree got a launch film, and Plank calls C. Millie, my phone assistant, shipped ten releases catching three bugs by calling herself. I split my labs into ten public repos and made Keyrate four versions sharing thirty tests.

## Oct 30

Kernel's release is on GitHub with chapters 1 to 4 from phone photos, and open issues went from 43 to zero.

I deleted six dead GitHub repos and set a rule: a project gets a repo once it has a store listing or domain. I picked eight apps for Product Hunt, weekly from Nov 13, and made big version jumps wait on me.

Airwell went public.

## Oct 31

Elah got reading plans on Today, and I fixed the iPad button. Airwell's new build says Airwell, not Windgate. Scrounger went back, and Oohaah, Cartons, Horula and Atlin still wait. Hermes is Cyllene now.

Elah's first iPhone build was rejected. I cut the NIV and added nine public domain Bibles, offline. Both wait on review.

## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check and twenty apps got fixes. Keyrate went desktop, iPad and Homebrew.

Sighting, the lost pets app, is on the App Store. Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Co-Stanza reached the App Store too.

Epiphany dropped the growth calculator, skips transfers in review and takes budget income from paydays. Coda's listing reads in twenty more languages, and Atlin's REW and Zillow feeds are live.

I ran Joshua Tree full screen on a real Raspberry Pi 4, and it was sharp. Board testing found five bugs, all fixed that afternoon; the eighth release passed with Bluetooth, a painted tree mark and an Apple-style boot.

Gaps against Berkeley's OS course became a list. The next release, with USB sticks, is ready on my Mac, and the window lag is fixed.

Nearly every landing runs the real product behind its hero, and I rigged my Vice character from my photo. He got real moves. The big one is Brookswood, rebuilt on real streets with Mount Baker on the horizon. The game is Apache 2.0 and live.

## Nov 2

I let Duke run Papers, Please overnight. Days 11 to 14 passed, but day 15 kept failing: a character died, a debt ended a run, and the bomb went off twice. The bot learned to defuse the bomb and check height and weight, then the Mac's text reader broke, so day 15 waits on a reboot.

Later Duke went back on Pikmin 2. It wiped its purple Pikmin three times in twenty-five minutes, so now it skips closed gates and saves before risky steps, then killed three Bulborbs and carried the Sunseed Berry home.
Joshémon, my Game Boy monster game, went public, then got abilities over six releases.

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube, and its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote by hand. Blackcomb plays MP4 video through decoders I wrote myself, so far only in the emulator.

Keyrate got its App Store record, free in 175 countries with nothing collected.

I summarized Kernel's last 88 book photos.

Vancouver Vice built its first packaged Mac app, with our own typeface, Seawall.

## Nov 3
I paused Duke's Pikmin 2 run at the end of Day 7. A cave run stalled, so it waits. Epiphany's statement upload button moved into the portfolio header on iPhone, with a Statements tab on the Mac. Joshemon has five releases ready for me to cut. I rebuilt the App Store status table from Apple's live data: nine apps are live on iPhone and Mac, and eleven have a rejection to answer.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes into roadmaps and the vault, and built a skill for mail. I got into school for Spring 2027. I put eleven more book chapters into Kernel, from four books, then deleted the 184 photos they came from. Five hundred and sixty-six photos are still waiting. Kernel moved photo summaries to Gemma 4 before the old model retires, and voice downloads survive storage limits. Bugle decodes every character in article text, and its hero has three big photo stories and no made-up bias numbers. Atlin's web map loads more cities as you pan. The iOS fixes for Kernel, Bugle and Epiphany need a store build.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam on iPhone and Mac. Its word list was shared with two big typing tests and its subtitle sounded like other apps, so I wrote my own from the 1,000 most common words in twenty public domain novels. The results screen now lights the five keys you miss most. Both are back in review.

I moved Hackrange, my pentesting dojo, out of the private vault into its own public repo after checking the history. It runs real attacks against Juice Shop on my own Mac, and a benchmark scores each solve against the app's own scoreboard, so the 19 solves are real. The landing is live at hackrange.jaybulb.com, and its rules of engagement bind every agent to localhost and training platforms only. The landing hero now types out a real pentest session and loops.
