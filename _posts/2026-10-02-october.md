## Oct 1-3

Joshua Tree's second release sandboxed twenty-six apps; Bookrank works offline on iPhone. 

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review. Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses.

## Oct 5-8

Apple rejected seven apps as spam, and Hikko over screenshots.

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1080p once I wrote the Wi-Fi login its chip lacked.

## Oct 11-13

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X; Plank can call models. Joshua Tree has a landing.

## Oct 16-18

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, fixed; jaybulb.com mail works at every address. All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Mailbag and Patois went into review, and I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31.

## Oct 19-21

Notate's offline update is live on iPhone and Watch; it became Echo. Glyphbox became Icelus, Temida's Apple sign-in fix cleared, Intake was taken so it's Machaon, and Curvely went into review as Pan. The calling app became Oohaah after three names.

Thinkpond became Melete, and Hermes sends nothing without my yes. Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 exam, and wrong word problems fell from 46 to 2. NYC Survive is Gastown now: a crew, a Steam Clock, no raids or cash.

## Oct 22-25

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so US plans stop hitting my Canadian total. Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused that, so the store says BugleNews. Clock became Horula, and five Apple appeals won.

## Oct 26-27

Duke beat my desert platformer Mirage in 3,725 steps, and I booked eight Product Hunt launches for Oct 28 to Nov 6 and appealed Cartons' spam rejection. Curbfind became Scrounger, since Apple refused Scrounge.

Noema's big day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations.

## Oct 28-29

Apple cleared my Sidewise spam answers, so I folded it into Bugle, live on iPhone and Mac. Wordroot became Eponym, Homeward became Sighting. Kernel went into App Review with a sleep timer and three narration languages.

Joshua Tree got a launch film, and Plank calls C. Millie, my phone assistant, shipped ten releases with spam blocking and caller memory, catching three bugs by calling herself. I split my labs into ten public repos and made Keyrate four versions sharing thirty tests.

## Oct 30

Kernel's release is on GitHub, with chapters 1 to 4 from phone photos; open issues went from 43 to zero.

I deleted six dead GitHub repos and set a rule: a project gets a repo once it has a store listing or domain. I picked eight apps for Product Hunt, weekly from Nov 13, and made big version jumps wait on me.

Airwell went public.

## Oct 31

Elah got reading plans on Today, and I fixed the iPad button. I filed thirteen Apple Notes into roadmaps, then emptied Notes. Airwell's new build says Airwell, not Windgate. Scrounger went back, and Oohaah, Cartons, Horula and Atlin still wait. Hermes is Cyllene now; its iPhone app waits for review.

Elah's first iPhone build was rejected, and Apple sign-in is throttled. I cut the NIV and added nine public domain Bibles, offline, with a picker in app and web. Both wait on review.

## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check and twenty apps got fixes. Nimble got Reduce Motion, a Linux package and two widgets. Keyrate went desktop, iPad and Homebrew; Noema's proofs now build one at a time, since CI ran out of memory.

Sighting, the lost and found pets app, is on the App Store. Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Co-Stanza reached the App Store too.

Epiphany dropped the growth calculator, skips transfers in review and takes budget income from paydays. Coda's listing reads in twenty more languages, and Atlin's REW and Zillow feeds are live.

I ran Joshua Tree full screen on a real Raspberry Pi 4 for the first time, and it was sharp. Board testing found five bugs, all fixed that afternoon; the eighth release passed with Bluetooth, a painted tree mark and an Apple-style boot. It stays awake showing stock prices and weather.

Gaps against Berkeley's OS course became the Berkeley list: threads with locks, all four Pi cores, pipes, apps that wait on other apps, sockets, and ten-millisecond slices so a busy app can't freeze the desktop. The next release, with USB sticks, is ready on my Mac. The window and dock lag is fixed.

Nearly every landing runs the real product behind its hero, and I rigged my Vice character from my photo. Tonight he got real moves: running, jumping, and a death fall when you get wasted. The big one is Brookswood, rebuilt on real streets with Mount Baker on the horizon. The game is Apache 2.0 and live.

## Nov 2

I let Duke run Papers, Please overnight. Days 11 to 14 went through, but day 15 kept failing: the family died once, a debt ended a run, and the bomb went off twice. The bot learned to defuse the bomb, hand the EZIC messenger his papers and let inspect mode check height and weight. Then the Mac's built-in text reader broke machine-wide, so day 15 waits on a reboot.

Later I put Duke back on Pikmin 2, streaming live on Twitch with the game's sound. It wiped its purple Pikmin three times in twenty-five minutes, so now it skips closed gates, names enemies from the game's own code and saves before every risky step. Then it killed three Bulborbs with no losses and carried the Sunseed Berry home. Day 5 is saved at 5 of 201 treasures and 940 Poko, about 146 bot hours to go. I also set Claude Code to nudge me every 35 minutes and compact sooner.

Joshémon, my Game Boy monster game, went public, then got abilities, real moves and power-cut-proof saves over six releases. Its landing plays the game behind the title through old TV scanlines.

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube today, and its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote by hand. Blackcomb plays MP4 video through decoders I wrote myself. So far that has only run in the emulator.

Keyrate got its App Store record: free in 175 countries, with a privacy label saying nothing is collected. The icon is now a staggered keyboard, since the old outline keys read as 888, and light mode is white everywhere. iPhone, iPad and Mac builds ship now.

I summarized Kernel's last 88 book photos after an old owner ID failed every upload, and cut two duplicate chapters.

Vancouver Vice built its first packaged Mac app. Title, save slots and tutorial passed QA, and a punch no longer freezes me. I gave it our own typeface, Seawall. The Lower Mainland runs at 60 frames a second with cops who chase you, but feels laggy and the art is so-so. Next: a mission inside a store, guns, more cops as stars rise, and car damage.
