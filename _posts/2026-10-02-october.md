## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree's second release sandboxed twenty-six apps; Bookrank works offline on iPhone. Auto-trading is live.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review. Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5 to 8

Apple rejected seven apps as spam, and Hikko over screenshots.

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1080p once I wrote the Wi-Fi login its chip lacked. I flashed the Pi card for Joshua Tree's book.

## Oct 11 to 13

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X; Plank can call models. Joshua Tree's landing is live.

## Oct 16 to 18

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, now fixed; jaybulb.com mail works at every address. All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Mailbag and Patois went into review. I also cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history.

## Oct 19 to 21

Notate's offline update is live on iPhone and Watch. Glyphbox became Icelus; its App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now, Thinkpond became Melete, and Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect, sends nothing without my yes. Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam; wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

The calling app became Oohaah after three names, both in review. Notate became Echo. Patois uses its new name and its streak-freeze crash is fixed; Temida's Apple sign-in fix cleared. Intake was taken, so it's Machaon. Curvely's second release went into review as Pan.

NYC Survive is Gastown now: a crew, a Steam Clock and self-running drills, no raids or cash. I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site without made-up prices, and Doorstock went to Apple.

## Oct 22 to 25

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so US plans stop hitting my Canadian total. Joshua Tree's core shrank by half, and Samantha answers on the Pi without my Mac.

Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused Bugle; the store says BugleNews. Clock became Horula. Five Apple appeals won; Atlin's store name is back.

## Oct 26 to 27

I made Mirage, a desert platformer Duke beat in 3,725 steps. I booked eight Product Hunt launches for Oct 28 to Nov 6 and appealed Cartons' spam rejection. Kaggle suspended my account; training waits on appeal. Curbfind became Scrounger, since Apple refused Scrounge.

Noema's biggest day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and C matched it. ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations and 61 confidence intervals in 52 trials with a lost minus sign.

## Oct 28 to 29

Apple cleared my Sidewise spam answers, so I pulled it into Bugle. Bugle's iPhone and Mac builds are live. Epiphany's news thumbnails shipped.

Wordroot became Eponym. Homeward became Sighting. Kernel went into App Review, then added a sleep timer, three narration languages, Goodreads sign-in and AI-free quizzes.

Placid Platypus is known through 181; I opened the AI lane: 169 of 182 SWE-bench Verified submissions can't be checked. 

Joshua Tree got a launch film, and Plank calls C. Millie, my phone assistant, shipped ten releases with spam blocking and caller memory, and caught three bugs by calling her own number.

I split my labs into ten public repos; GitHub's limit holds four more back. I turned Keyrate from one web page into four versions sharing thirty tests.

## Oct 30

Kernel's release is on GitHub, with chapters 1 to 4 from phone photos; open issues went from 43 to zero.

The map has rain radar and storm tracks. Temida stops panning on iPhone, and I fixed the Noema links the labs split broke.

I renamed the Bible app Elah, since Apple had the plain name; it gained highlights, notes, search and listening. I deleted six dead GitHub repos and wrote a rule: a project gets a repo once it has a store listing or domain. I sent 34 Apple Notes into 29 roadmaps, and picked eight of eighteen unlaunched apps for Product Hunt, weekly from Nov 13.

I gave Joshua Tree's Dock a Magnify setting. I tightened the rules: big jumps wait on me, features are minors, fixes are patches, shipped numbers never change.

Tripwire covers every repo, but it burned most of an AI credit and misses most of what's left. Shipped by hand: Temida's money bars stopped animating and every headline uses one font; and Airwell went public.

## Oct 31

Millie quotes my status and texts back, and booked a family movie for Sunday. Elah got reading plans on Today. I fixed the iPad button.

I filed thirteen Apple Notes into roadmaps and the wiki, then emptied Notes. Epiphany's charts got five-year and all-time ranges, and crypto prices stream about once a second.

Every open rejection got an answer or a new build. Patois now signs in through my own server, fixing Apple's Sign in with Apple error. Airwell's new build says Airwell, not the old Windgate build Apple reviewed. Scrounger went back with newer features, Blackcomb answered Apple's nine questions, and Oohaah, Cartons, Horula and Atlin wait on Apple.

Hermes is Cyllene now, bundle id aside. Its iPhone app got App Lock, a TestFlight tab and a What's New translator. I also gave it sales at a glance, a share card and a launch film for its first major version. Those wait for the current version's review.

Elah's first iPhone build was rejected, and Apple sign-in is throttled. I cut the NIV and put nine public domain Bibles in as real text, offline, with a picker in the app and on the web. The headline is now Read the Bible, anywhere. Both are waiting for review.

## Nov 1

Spent the day on accessibility. I ran an automated check on all 60 landing pages and all 38 web app pages, and every one now comes back clean; about twenty apps got fixes. The shared jaybulb.com grey went up to 4.9 to 1, so faint text stops failing for everyone. I also fixed the dead links it found.

Keyrate got a desktop app, a Test menu, an iPad layout and a live terminal version. Co-Stanza can now edit a poem in place, take kudos that only the author can count, follow other poets, and read a Yours feed on the web. Noema's proof check kept running out of memory on the CI runner, so it builds the big proofs one at a time now.

Later I merged a fix that keeps Echo's dictation warm, and Echo's landing now leads with its tagline. Epiphany dropped the growth calculator, its iPhone ticker swipes with Fear and Greed underneath, and its review queue skips deposits and transfers while re-sorting old rows. Machaon moved to the system font, Nimble's mascot became a lightbulb with a face, and Scrounger's deal search stopped timing out. Airwell, Numen, Plank and Turing got house-style landings, and every hero headline now shows the tagline instead of the app name. Keyrate's terminal app is on Homebrew.
