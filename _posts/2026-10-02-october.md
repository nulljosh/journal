## Oct 1-3

Joshua Tree's second release sandboxed twenty-six apps; Bookrank works offline on iPhone.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review. Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses.

## Oct 5-8

Apple rejected seven apps as spam, Hikko over screenshots.

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1080p once I wrote its Wi-Fi login.

## Oct 11-13

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X; Plank can call models.

## Oct 16-18

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, fixed; jaybulb.com mail works at every address. All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Mailbag and Patois went into review, and I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31.

## Oct 19-21

Notate's offline update is live on iPhone and Watch; it became Echo. Glyphbox became Icelus, Intake was taken so it's Machaon, and Curvely went into review as Pan.

Thinkpond became Melete, and Hermes sends nothing without my yes. Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 exam. NYC Survive is Gastown now.

## Oct 22-25

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so US plans stop hitting my Canadian total. Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused that, so the store says BugleNews. Clock became Horula, and five Apple appeals won.

## Oct 26-27

Duke beat my platformer Mirage in 3,725 steps. I booked eight Product Hunt launches for Oct 28 to Nov 6 and appealed Cartons' spam rejection. Curbfind became Scrounger.

Noema's big day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations.

## Oct 28-29

Apple cleared Sidewise's spam answers, and it folded into Bugle, live on iPhone and Mac. Wordroot became Eponym, Homeward became Sighting. Kernel went into App Review.

Joshua Tree got a film, and Plank calls C. Millie, my phone assistant, shipped ten releases catching three bugs by calling herself. I split my labs into ten public repos and made Keyrate four versions sharing thirty tests.

## Oct 30

Kernel's release is on GitHub with chapters 1 to 4 from phone photos, and open issues went from 43 to zero.

I deleted six dead GitHub repos and set a rule: a project gets a repo once it has a store listing or domain. Big version jumps now wait on me.

Airwell went public.

## Oct 31

Airwell's new build drops Windgate. Scrounger went back; Oohaah, Cartons, Horula and Atlin still wait. Hermes is Cyllene now.

Elah's first iPhone build was rejected, so I cut the NIV and added nine public domain Bibles, offline. Both wait on review.

## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check and twenty apps got fixes.

Sighting, the lost pets app, and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Epiphany dropped the growth calculator, skips transfers in review and takes budget income from paydays. Coda's listing reads in twenty more languages, and Atlin's REW and Zillow feeds are live.

Joshua Tree ran full screen on a real Raspberry Pi 4, and it was sharp. Board testing found five bugs, all fixed that afternoon; the eighth release passed with Bluetooth, a painted tree mark and an Apple-style boot. The USB-stick release is ready on my Mac.

I rigged my Vice character from my photo, and he got real moves. Brookswood, the big one, is rebuilt on real streets with Mount Baker on the horizon, and it's live under Apache 2.0.

## Nov 2

I let Duke run Papers, Please overnight. It got through day 14 and learned to defuse the bomb, then day 15 waits on a reboot after the Mac's text reader broke.

Later Duke went back on Pikmin 2. It wiped its purple Pikmin three times in twenty-five minutes, so now it skips closed gates and saves before risky steps; it killed three Bulborbs and carried the Sunseed Berry home. Joshémon, my Game Boy monster game, went public, then got abilities over six releases.

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube, and its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote by hand. Blackcomb plays MP4 through my own decoders, so far only in the emulator.

Keyrate got its App Store record, free in 175 countries with nothing collected. Vancouver Vice built its first packaged Mac app, with our own typeface, Seawall.

## Nov 3

Epiphany's statement upload button moved into the iPhone portfolio header, and the Mac gets a Statements tab. The App Store status table is rebuilt from Apple's live data: nine apps are live on iPhone and Mac, and eleven have a rejection to answer.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes into roadmaps and the vault, and built a mail skill. I got into school for Spring 2027. I put eleven more book chapters into Kernel from four books, then deleted the 184 source photos; 566 are still waiting.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam on both platforms, since its word list matched two big typing tests. I rebuilt the list from the 1,000 most common words in twenty public domain novels, and both are back in review.

I moved Hackrange, my pentesting dojo, public. It runs real attacks on Juice Shop locally and scores each solve, and its rules keep every agent on localhost and training platforms.

## Nov 7

Cyllene is back before Apple with a read-only reviewer key, answers and a demo video; twelve apps wait on review. Its Resolve All picker takes one app, a couple, or all of them. CNN blocks Cloudflare, so Epiphany's Fear and Greed gauge sat blank until it fell back to its own market mood score. Nine projects broke under Xcode 27 and are fixed. I installed Apple's Xcode plugin for Claude Code, so Claude can build, run and test inside Xcode.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. I answered all six review threads with app-specific replies, the same move that got Horula and Atlin through, and now I wait on Apple. I killed Oohaah outright, worker, repo and folder, and scrubbed it from the docs and portfolio, since Apple bans random video chat apps anyway. I also made a hook that sends long commands to the background on its own.

Nimble's fix for "What's 9+10" getting answered by a Wikipedia article about gliders cleared Apple tonight, after my iPhone sat on the old build. Bugle is keeping its name, Bugles: I checked about 200 alternatives against Apple's duplicate check, and every plain news word is taken. Coda stays Quotestreak, since its cousins are taken too.

My Dock still showed a stale black globe from an old store copy, so I pointed it at the fresh build and resubmitted without the old names. After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, address suggestions, site icons, a fire button, HTTPS first, tab overviews and on-device translation. iPhone is still in review.

My menu bar's Claude usage number sat stale because Anthropic kept turning away the bar and hook. Both share one copy now, showing session and weekly together, with an age tag past ten minutes.
