## Oct 1-3

Joshua Tree's second release sandboxed twenty-six apps; Bookrank works offline.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.
## Oct 5-8

Apple rejected seven apps as spam, Hikko over screenshots.

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1080p once I wrote its Wi-Fi login.

## Oct 11-13

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X; Plank can call models.

## Oct 16-18

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, fixed; jaybulb.com mail works at info@.
A security header broke fonts on my pages, so I fixed ten repos. Mailbag and Patois went into review, and I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31.

## Oct 19-21

Notate's offline update is live on iPhone and Watch; it became Echo. Glyphbox became Icelus, and Curvely went into review as Pan.

Thinkpond became Melete, and Hermes sends nothing without my yes.

## Oct 22-25

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so US plans stop hitting my Canadian total. Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused, so the store says BugleNews. Five Apple appeals won.

## Oct 26-27

Duke beat my platformer Mirage in 3,725 steps. I booked eight Product Hunt launches and appealed Cartons' spam rejection.
Noema's big day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations.

## Oct 28-29

Apple cleared Sidewise's spam answers, and it folded into Bugle, live on iPhone and Mac. Homeward became Sighting. Kernel went into App Review.

Joshua Tree got a film, and Plank calls C. Millie, my phone assistant, shipped ten releases catching three bugs by calling herself. I split my labs into ten public repos and made Keyrate four versions sharing thirty tests.

## Oct 30

Kernel's release is on GitHub with chapters 1 to 4 from phone photos; open issues went from 43 to zero.

I deleted six dead GitHub repos and set a rule: a repo needs a store listing or domain.

## Oct 31

Airwell's new build drops Windgate. Scrounger went back; Cartons, Horula and Atlin still wait.
Elah's first iPhone build was rejected, so I cut the NIV and added nine public domain Bibles, offline; both wait on review.

## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check, and twenty apps got fixes.

Sighting and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Epiphany dropped the growth calculator, skips transfers in review and takes budget income from paydays.
Joshua Tree ran full screen on a real Raspberry Pi 4, sharp. Board testing found five bugs, fixed that afternoon; the eighth release passed with Bluetooth, a painted tree mark and an Apple-style boot.

Brookswood, the big one, is rebuilt on real streets, Mount Baker on the horizon.

## Nov 2

I let Duke run Papers, Please overnight. It got through day 14 and learned to defuse the bomb; day 15 waits on a reboot after the Mac's text reader broke.

Later Duke went back to Pikmin 2 and wiped its purple Pikmin three times in twenty-five minutes, so it now saves first. Joshemon, my Game Boy game, went public, then got six ability releases.

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube, and its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote by hand.
Keyrate got its App Store record, free in 175 countries with nothing collected. Vancouver Vice built its first packaged Mac app, with our own typeface, Seawall.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes into roadmaps and the vault, and built a mail skill. I put eleven more book chapters into Kernel from four books and deleted 184 source photos; 566 still wait.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam on both platforms; its word list matched two big typing tests. I rebuilt it from the 1,000 most common words in twenty public domain novels; both are back in review.

## Nov 7

Cyllene is back before Apple with a read-only reviewer key and a demo video; twelve apps wait. Nine projects broke under Xcode 27; all fixed. I installed Apple's Xcode plugin, so Claude can build, run and test inside Xcode.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. I answered all six review threads with app-specific replies, which got Horula and Atlin through.
Nimble's fix for a glider article answering "What's 9+10" cleared Apple tonight. Bugle is keeping its name, Bugles, after checking about 200 others.

After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, site icons, a fire button, HTTPS first and translation. Mac is in review, iPhone waits.

## Nov 8

iPhone and Mac builds are back in review. Plaintxt's builds wait; Apple wants bigger iOS changes, and if it bounces again I drop iOS and keep the Mac app.

Cyllene's onboarding has four real screens and leads with Apple's own sign-in button, tested in the simulator; real login ships after the iPhone review.
Epiphany's broker numbers had been quietly stale: pull to refresh never got past the cache, and the "synced 2 minutes ago" label timed my own request, not the daily broker update.

## Nov 9

The iPhone and Mac update is with Apple for review, and the web is live. A refresh now means a refresh, synced holdings show how old the broker's data is, and cost basis and gains reach the apps through the server. Why holdings show as days old is still open.

Joshua Tree's next build is merged, with 32 issues closed. It adds PC window snapping, QEMU debugging, Terminal man pages, new apps on reused disks, Focus mode and Pi ping. I hit the session limit before PC ping, still open.

Epiphany's iPhone statement upload never worked, so my October statement missed the spending graph. The server took the same PDF sent directly, so the bug was in the phone app. The fix reads iCloud files properly and shows errors as a red row, untested on a real phone.

jaybulb.com email is live through iCloud+. A test from info@ came back. Only info@ receives so far.

The journal borrowed colours from an old stylesheet, four of which never existed, so some dividers and labels vanished. It has its own colour file now, and a QA pass fixed a doubled heading, uneven cards and overlapping chart labels.

## Nov 10

Hamurapi's dark-mode landing shows the game as a night scene, not grey mud. I redrew the sprites with legs, faces and two-stalk wheat, and cut a trailer from the web game. Hamurapi got approved on both platforms, and the price and store link are still to do.

Millie stopped treating me like a stranger. Texts from my own phone used to get her intro and an offer to pass my message along to me. Now she skips that, tells me what calls and texts came in over the week, and says whether quiet hours are on.

Apple rejected Patois for not signing in with Apple, which was never spam. The sheet stayed open after Apple sign-in, so nothing seemed to happen. That is fixed, and the iPhone build is back in review.
