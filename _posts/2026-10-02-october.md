Notate's offline update is live on iPhone and Watch. Glyphbox became Icelus; its App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now, with no case details in its public repo. Thinkpond became Melete.

## Oct 20

Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline; its confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon and all 45 sweep problems closed. The calling app became Oohaah after three names; both are in review.

Cartons went into App Review.

## Oct 21

Patois uses its new name everywhere; its streak-freeze crash is fixed. Temida's Sign in with Apple fix cleared on iPhone and Mac.

NYC Survive is Gastown now: a crew, a Steam Clock, self-running drills, no raids or cash. A headless bot proved it winnable in twelve days.

I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site without its made-up prices; Doorstock, my parts and jobs app, went to Apple.

## Oct 22

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so a US plan stops hitting my Canadian totals.

My Duolingo runner is Surf now; a disk cleanup killed it, but I saved the 197-day streak.

Apple rejected the first Cartons and Oohaah builds; Doorstock syncs shops and jobs.

## Oct 25

Joshua Tree's main source file shrank by half, and Samantha answers on the Pi without my Mac.

Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused plain Bugle, so the store says BugleNews.

Clock became Horula, since every clock name was taken; by night it had twelve languages.

Five Apple appeals won; Atlin's store name is back.

## Oct 26

I made Mirage, a desert platformer Duke beat in 3,725 steps; its Apple apps are building.

Patois's packs copy Duke's solved vocabulary, so Turkish grew from 11 units to 120. I booked eight Product Hunt launches for Oct 28 to Nov 6 and appealed Cartons' spam rejection.

Joshua Tree's next build is in CI with a sixth tuft and a three-minute Samantha cap; my board-pass card is flashed.

Kaggle suspended my account without a reason, so training waits on my appeal; Samantha now says what she can't do.

## Oct 27

Curbfind became Scrounger, since Apple wouldn't take plain Scrounge; the store still says Curbfind.

Icelus got transitions on web and Swift; its npm and registry launches wait on me.

Noema's biggest day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and a C program matched the published five-state halting-machine count. It may be a new OEIS term answering a 2020 Aaronson question; OEIS waits on an editor. In medicine, ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations and 61 confidence intervals in 52 trials with a lost minus sign.

## Oct 28

Apple cleared my Sidewise spam answers, so I pulled it and folded it into Bugle.

Bugle's iPhone and Mac builds are live, a newer Mac build in review. Epiphany's news thumbnails shipped, and MarketWatch went from 25 stories to 84.

Wordroot became Eponym; the store still says Wordroot.

Homeward became Sighting, my lost-pets app with a species filter, maps and pet alerts on web, phone and Mac.

Scrounger got category chips, saved searches, a map and price-drop badges.

Kernel went into App Review, then its loop ran late: a sleep timer, French, Spanish and German narration, Goodreads sign-in, and quizzes built from its own summaries, no AI.

I sent 17 App Store headers to review and answered four spam rejections.

Duke's Oxygen Not Included autopilot ate my afternoon: five colonies died by cycle 31, and the sixth runs algae-free.

## Oct 29

Placid Platypus went from known through 176 to 181, and searching beside a gap cracked 177, 178 and 188. I opened the AI lane: 169 of 182 SWE-bench Verified submissions cannot be checked. I machine-checked the weakest step of the triangular Ising proof; it matched two published numbers. Duke moved to Pikmin 2 on Twitch under an 8-bit brand and cleared Emergence Cave on Day 4.

Joshua Tree got a launch film, a page teaching strangers to code, a debugger that steps apps, and Plank calling C. USB sticks are built; the Pi is tested this weekend.

Epiphany's update is in App Review on iPhone and Mac, with live crypto prices, turning planes and a Watch complication queued. The web is live.

Secretary, my phone assistant Millie, shipped ten releases: call transcripts, spam blocking, a private inbox, caller memory, French and callbacks. Tonight she called her own number, caught three bugs (now fixed) and proved the new features live.

Kernel moved to kernel.jaybulb.com; my portfolio is one list of 48 projects.

I split my labs into ten public repos, archived two, and cleared Apple Notes to zero, filing six into roadmaps and wiki. Notes and brain went public with fresh histories; notes now feeds Obsidian and brain on every save. Coda, Bugle, Horula and Hamurapi are live App Store apps; GitHub's repo limit holds four more back.

I turned Keyrate from one web page into four versions; thirty shared test cases keep them alike.

## Oct 30

Overnight I built a run of Epiphany cards on web, iPhone and Mac, from bills and dividends to a greeting that knows what changed since my last visit. Next up: plane details, rain radar, insider trades.
