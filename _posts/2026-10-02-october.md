The map has rain radar, shaded warnings and hurricane tracks with forecast cones; Environment Canada's old feed died, so warnings come from its newer one. Planes filter by passenger, cargo, military or private, and stock pages show insider trades and who owns each stock. Epiphany now loops the last two hours of rain radar, flags when you're inside a warning, and shows how your holdings did against the S&P 500 today.

Mailbag got a San Francisco-style font, a mail-case icon and a mascot.

Bugle stopped showing one paper's copy of a story once I followed another, and Hacker News comment links work again. Noema's repo links went dead after the labs split; I fixed every one and repointed two OEIS drafts.

Hermes shipped four releases: an app icon, Liquid Glass on Resolve All for macOS 26, in-app events in new submissions, and its own saved Apple password. Its old majors were each one feature, so now features are minors and majors are my call. Ten Apple threads stay unread until I sign in.
