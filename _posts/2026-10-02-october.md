## Oct 1-3

Joshua Tree's second release sandboxed twenty-six apps; Bookrank works offline on iPhone.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.
## Oct 5-8

Apple rejected seven apps as spam, Hikko over screenshots.

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1080p once I wrote its Wi-Fi login.

## Oct 11-13

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X; Plank can call models.

## Oct 16-18

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, fixed; jaybulb.com mail works at every address.
A security header blocked my tokens file and pages fell back to Times New Roman, so I fixed ten repos. Mailbag and Patois went into review, and I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31.

## Oct 19-21

Notate's offline update is live on iPhone and Watch; it became Echo. Glyphbox became Icelus, and Curvely went into review as Pan.

Thinkpond became Melete, and Hermes sends nothing without my yes. Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 exam.
## Oct 22-25

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so US plans stop hitting my Canadian total. Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused, so the store says BugleNews. Five Apple appeals won.

## Oct 26-27

Duke beat my platformer Mirage in 3,725 steps. I booked eight Product Hunt launches and appealed Cartons' spam rejection.
Noema's big day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations.

## Oct 28-29

Apple cleared Sidewise's spam answers, and it folded into Bugle, live on iPhone and Mac. Homeward became Sighting. Kernel went into App Review.

Joshua Tree got a film, and Plank calls C. Millie, my phone assistant, shipped ten releases catching three bugs by calling herself. I split my labs into ten public repos and made Keyrate four versions sharing thirty tests.

## Oct 30

Kernel's release is on GitHub with chapters 1 to 4 from phone photos, and open issues went from 43 to zero.

I deleted six dead GitHub repos and set a rule: a project gets one once it has a store listing or domain.

## Oct 31

Airwell's new build drops Windgate. Scrounger went back; Oohaah, Cartons, Horula and Atlin still wait.
Elah's first iPhone build was rejected, so I cut the NIV and added nine public domain Bibles, offline. Both wait on review.

## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check, and twenty apps got fixes.

Sighting, the lost pets app, and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Epiphany dropped the growth calculator, skips transfers in review and takes budget income from paydays. Coda's listing is in twenty more languages, and Atlin's REW and Zillow feeds are live.

Joshua Tree ran full screen on a real Raspberry Pi 4, sharp. Board testing found five bugs, fixed that afternoon; the eighth release passed with Bluetooth, a painted tree mark and an Apple-style boot. The USB-stick release is ready on my Mac.

Brookswood, the big one, is rebuilt on real streets with Mount Baker on the horizon, live under Apache 2.0.

## Nov 2

I let Duke run Papers, Please overnight. It got through day 14 and learned to defuse the bomb; day 15 waits on a reboot after the Mac's text reader broke.

Later Duke went back on Pikmin 2. It wiped its purple Pikmin three times in twenty-five minutes, so now it skips closed gates and saves before risky steps. Joshemon, my Game Boy monster game, went public, then got six ability releases.

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube, and its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote by hand. Blackcomb plays MP4 through my own decoders, so far only in the emulator.

Keyrate got its App Store record, free in 175 countries with nothing collected. Vancouver Vice built its first packaged Mac app, with our own typeface, Seawall.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes into roadmaps and the vault, and built a mail skill. I put eleven more book chapters into Kernel from four books, then deleted the 184 source photos; 566 are still waiting.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam on both platforms; its word list matched two big typing tests. I rebuilt it from the 1,000 most common words in twenty public domain novels; both are back in review.

## Nov 7

Cyllene is back before Apple with a read-only reviewer key, answers and a demo video; twelve apps wait. Nine projects broke under Xcode 27 and are fixed. I installed Apple's Xcode plugin, so Claude can build, run and test inside Xcode.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. I answered all six review threads with app-specific replies, the move that got Horula and Atlin through. I killed Oohaah outright, repo, worker and folder, since Apple bans random video chat apps.

Nimble's fix for a Wikipedia article about gliders answering "What's 9+10" cleared Apple tonight. Bugle is keeping its name, Bugles, after I checked about 200 others.

After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, address suggestions, site icons, a fire button, HTTPS first, tab overviews and on-device translation. The Mac version is in review, iPhone still waits, tab groups come next.

## Nov 8

The iPhone and Mac builds are back in review. Plaintxt's iPhone and Mac builds wait too, with encoding kept and a footer picker; Apple wants bigger changes on iOS, and if it bounces again, I drop iOS and keep the Mac app.

Cyllene's onboarding now has four real screens and leads sign-in with Apple's own button. I tested it in the simulator; real Apple login ships after the iPhone review. Coda and Bugle wait too. I closed 18 finished GitHub issues and merged 39 duplicates, taking the open count from 518 to 461.

Epiphany's broker numbers had been quietly stale. Pull to refresh never got past the cache, and the "synced 2 minutes ago" label was timing my own request, while the brokerage updates once a day.

## Nov 9

The iPhone and Mac update is with Apple for review, and the web is live. A refresh now means a refresh, synced holdings show how old the broker's data is, and cost basis and gains reach the apps through the server. I still don't know why holdings show as days old, so that stays open.

Joshua Tree's next build is merged; I closed 32 issues, mostly shipped. It adds PC window snapping, QEMU debugging on both ports, a Pi green dot filling the screen, man pages for Terminal commands, a temp command, new apps on reused disks, app notices, Focus mode and Pi ping. I hit the session limit before PC ping, still open.

Epiphany's iPhone statement upload had never worked reliably. My October bank statement never reached the server, so it missed the spending graph. The PDF was fine, and the live server took the same file when I sent it directly, so the bug is in the phone app. The fix reads the file the way iCloud files need and shows upload errors as a red row, not a popup iOS can drop. I haven't tried it on a real phone yet.
