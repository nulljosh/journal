## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check; twenty apps got fixes. Sighting and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Joshua Tree first ran full screen on a real Raspberry Pi 4; board testing found five bugs, all fixed by afternoon.

## Nov 2

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube; its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes, added eleven Kernel chapters from four books, and deleted 184 source photos; 566 wait.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam over two typing tests; I rebuilt its word list from public-domain novels and both are back in review.

## Nov 7

Nine projects broke under Xcode 27; all are fixed.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, site icons, a fire button and translation. Mac is in review, iPhone waits.

## Nov 9

The iPhone and Mac update is in review; the web is live.

## Nov 10

Hamurapi's trailer is approved everywhere.

Apple rejected Patois for not signing in with Apple, which was never spam. Fixed; the iPhone build is back in review.

## Nov 11

Typed math answers count in any order, and math courses show x², √2 and π. Pre-Calc 11 went from ten to sixty questions, and 12 explains every answer.

## Nov 13

Apple locked my account until I accepted a new agreement. Tocks was rejected for background audio, and Apple asked what Delete account does; lost-pet posts go too. Co-Stanza is live on iPhone and Mac.

## Nov 14

Sent an iPhone update to Apple. The Nimble name belongs to another developer.

## Nov 15

The Pi's auto lock is in review: only my password opens it.

I built the three pieces Doorstock's spec lacked: spring costs, a label scanner and a monthly accountant CSV. Mac is built; iPhone waits on appeal. Talli got a money log with a yearly limit bar, live on web and server.

Kernel imports a book you own and summarizes each chapter. Epiphany is free on the web; Pro is $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year, and anyone who paid $1 keeps it for life. I shut three billing holes; the iPhone paywall is next. I killed Gato and the CanLII app; Temida does that search.

## Nov 16

Board night ran past midnight; a stale card with the wrong clock broke every secure download. Paddle froze because every frame repainted everything; now it repaints only what moved. I started a carrier board for the Compute Module 5.

Joshua Tree's version 14 shipped graphics acceleration; the Mac now drives it. I started a 64-bit PC port. Samantha's captioner writes captions by listening; the first test was word for word. Her trailer is done. The LaCie is gone, taking the 9B model's data with it.
## Nov 17

Keyrate got a new icon and a drill mode built from the keys I miss most; both are in review.

Samantha's video tools are done. The landing opens on her trailer; scrolling no longer kills the live iPhone demo.

## Nov 18

The landing lost its typewriter headline. A new desktop section shows Spaces, Mission Control, Spotlight and Launchpad.

I graded the Joshua Tree landing a B-, cut its repeats and made the desktop clips one swipeable strip. Turing's picker reached its major release. A fresh sealed set of 420 rows passed all three bars; a new guard refuses about half as many right answers. Both sealed sets are spent.

Vancouver Vice's Unreal project lived only on the LaCie, so the city and builds are gone; rebuilding from the web game and map data.

## Nov 21

Airwell passed App Review and went live. I fixed two API bugs: cancels errored and failures got cached.

Filters ruined the desert wallpaper, so 0.25 credits bought a real wax-crayon painting. Joshua graded it A; open: a true 16:9 cut and a quiet dock.

A security audit of every server app found four real holes. Epiphany's CDN cached one person's private data by URL, readable by anyone for an hour; fixed. Password reset links could open any app link, so one tap could take over an account; now only https opens. Talli let any signed-in user read others' files, and Melete could be tricked into linking a squatted signup; all four shipped. Turing's public voice endpoint still lets anyone make his cloned voice say any text; locking it waits on me.

Apple said Epiphany's iPhone build had ads; it has none, so I resubmitted. Sighting's notes now cover account deletion; it's back in. Scrounger was rejected for user posts; it got a terms screen with Report and Block. Elah was rejected twice for spam; I resubmitted with a new map and icon.

I read 43 saved X posts and cleared them. They graded B, strong on tools and thin on original ideas, and now seed Melete's daily idea.

## Nov 22

The Connect Mouse shortcut froze for fifteen seconds at a time, so I rebuilt it as Tether, a menu bar app that revives a mouse. A review found ten problems; I fixed eight. Hormuz got the Keyrate look; the strait is still shut, day 268.

## Nov 23

I shipped Echo's new release with Word export and a Mac dictation hotkey, and rebuilt its landing around the live view. Patois got its parrot icon back, and I redrew the 32 Things icons as metal, glass and wood. Most saved X ideas were already built or not worth it. I asked Apple for access to its bigger cloud model; Nimble, Echo, Cyllene and Plaintxt get it once it lands.

I sorted 87 Apple Notes into 25 roadmaps, then emptied Notes. A pull wiped my terminal settings; GitHub restored them. My sticker proof is approved and in production.

Kernel got a big batch of books: Hackers and Painters is whole, Claude AI for Dummies has chapters 4 to 16, Video Game Design for Dummies has 13 and 14, and Einstein and Relativity is new. Plank got its book. I rewrote the guide in No Starch style, with a big chapter number, a Bruegel painting and a Try It Yourself box per chapter. The first pass added seven drawn figures and a summary per chapter; the PDF is live.

Scrounger's grey listing photos and wrong city names are fixed.

## Nov 25

I checked the App Store numbers, saw almost nobody buying, and made nine small apps free as an experiment, with no paywall or in-app purchase. Echo, Kernel and Epiphany stay as they are. On December 23 I'll read the downloads to see whether price or discovery was the problem.

I spent the rest of the day making my apps work with bigger text and screen readers. Text in seventeen apps now grows with the text size setting, and the icon-only buttons say what they do out loud. I wrote an iOS guide and a checker for it. Machaon's lab charts get a spoken summary now. I'm going back to KPU in January, the classes are in my calendar, and the student aid paperwork is done.
