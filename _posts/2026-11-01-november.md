## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check and twenty apps got fixes. Sighting and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Joshua Tree first ran full screen on a Pi 4; board testing found five bugs, fixed.

## Nov 2

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube; its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes, added eleven Kernel chapters from four books, and deleted 184 source photos; 566 wait.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam over two typing tests; I rebuilt its word list from public-domain novels, and it's back in review.

## Nov 7

Nine projects broke under Xcode 27 and are fixed.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, site icons, a fire button and translation. Mac is in review, iPhone waits.

## Nov 10

Apple rejected Patois for not signing in with Apple, which was never spam. Fixed; the iPhone build is back in review.

## Nov 11

Typed math answers count in any order, and math courses show x², √2 and π. Pre-Calc 11 went from ten to sixty questions, and 12 explains every answer.

## Nov 13

Apple locked my account until I accepted a new agreement. Tocks was rejected for background audio, and Apple asked what Delete account does; lost-pet posts go too. Co-Stanza is live on iPhone and Mac.

## Nov 14

Sent an iPhone update to Apple, but another developer has the Nimble name.

## Nov 15

The Pi's auto lock is in review: only my password opens it.

I built the three pieces Doorstock's spec lacked: spring costs, a label scanner and an accountant CSV. Mac is built; iPhone waits on appeal. Talli got a money log with a yearly limit bar, live on web and server.

Epiphany is free on the web; Pro is $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year, and anyone who paid $1 keeps it for life. I shut three billing holes; the iPhone paywall is next. I killed Gato and the CanLII app; Temida does that search.

## Nov 16

A stale card clock broke secure downloads. Paddle froze because every frame repainted everything; now only moving parts do. I started a carrier board for the Compute Module 5.

Joshua Tree shipped graphics acceleration; the Mac now drives it. I started a 64-bit PC port. Samantha's captioner wrote captions by listening, word for word.
## Nov 17

Keyrate got a new icon and a drill mode from the keys I miss most.
## Nov 18

A new desktop section shows Spaces, Mission Control, Spotlight and Launchpad.

I graded the Joshua Tree landing a B-, cut its repeats and made the desktop clips one swipe strip. Turing's picker reached its major release. A fresh sealed set of 420 rows passed all three bars.

Vancouver Vice's Unreal project was lost with the LaCie; I'm rebuilding it from the web game and map data.

## Nov 21

Airwell passed App Review and went live. I fixed two API bugs: cancels errored and failures got cached.

Filters ruined the desert wallpaper, so a wax-crayon painting replaced it.

A security audit of every server app found four real holes, now shipped. Epiphany's CDN cached one person's private data by URL for an hour; password reset links could open any app link, so one tap could take over an account; Talli let signed-in users read others' files; Melete could link a squatted signup. Turing's public voice endpoint still lets anyone make his cloned voice say any text; locking it is mine to do.

Apple said Epiphany's iPhone build had ads; it has none, so I resubmitted. Scrounger was rejected for user posts; it got a terms screen with Report and Block. Elah was rejected twice for spam; I resubmitted with a new map and icon.

I cleared 43 saved X posts; they graded B, strong on tools, thin on ideas, and seed Melete's idea.

## Nov 22

The Connect Mouse shortcut froze for fifteen seconds, so I rebuilt it as Tether, a menu bar app that revives a mouse. A review found ten problems; I fixed eight. Hormuz got the Keyrate look; the strait is still shut, day 268.

## Nov 23

I shipped Echo's release with Word export and a Mac dictation hotkey, and rebuilt its landing around the live view. Patois got its parrot icon back, and I redrew the 32 Things icons. I asked Apple for its bigger cloud model, which Nimble, Echo, Cyllene and Plaintxt get once it lands.

I sorted 87 Apple Notes into 25 roadmaps and emptied Notes. My sticker proof is approved and in production.

Kernel got more books: Hackers and Painters is whole, Claude AI for Dummies has chapters 4 to 16, Video Game Design for Dummies has 13 and 14, and Einstein and Relativity is new.
Scrounger's grey photos and wrong city names are fixed.

## Nov 25

Almost nobody was buying, so I made nine small apps free as an experiment, leaving Echo, Kernel and Epiphany alone. On December 23 I'll check whether price or discovery was the problem.

Airwell got the biggest push: themes, a welcome screen and eleven languages, checked in German and Arabic at the largest text size. The update is with Apple; the Product Hunt launch waits.

I cleaned up App Store Connect: deleted Oohaah and Sidewise, and dropped China from all 21 live apps to keep Apple's China AI and security banners off. Apple's reasons are in: Elah and Cartons are spam, Mailbag and Scrounger got 2.1 issues, Sighting got an account deletion question, and Plaintxt's Mac version is approved.

Epiphany's copy had fallen weeks behind GitHub, so I reapplied the lost fixes. Its US Get Pro button, linking to Stripe, is with Apple; once approved, the app goes free and sells Pro on the web.

I spent the rest of the day on bigger text and screen readers: seventeen apps grow with text size, and icon buttons speak.

## Nov 26

Our uptime check pinged a dead site every half hour; I removed it. Blackcomb's welcome screen, fade-in and start page are waiting on Apple. I'm taking math at KPU in January.

Bugle's landing got rebuilt around a live feed, and its App Store copy leads with news.

I built a promo tool that cuts YouTube walkthroughs and TikTok ads from real screens. Ten apps have videos; six wait for a retake because their screenshots show old names.

Flamingo Map got its App Store listing. Cyllene's reply drafter now falls back to a local model, then Claude, splits Apple's numbered questions, and holds back answers only I can give. The Flamingo reply is drafted except the screen recording, which is on a third take after two missed the tab bar. Nothing has gone to Apple. Cyllene's iPhone app is live.

Product Hunt took the rest of the day. I went through all 32 products, fixed pages with old names, and put back a price a script had blanked. Fourteen apps without a launch got kits; Costanza is ready to schedule. Nothing is scheduled.
