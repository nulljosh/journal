## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check; twenty apps got fixes. Sighting and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Joshua Tree first ran full screen on a real Raspberry Pi 4; board testing found five bugs, fixed by afternoon, and release eight passed.

## Nov 2

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube; its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes, added eleven Kernel chapters from four books, and deleted 184 source photos; 566 wait.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam over two typing tests. I rebuilt its word list from public-domain novels; both are back in review.

## Nov 7

Cyllene is back before Apple. Nine projects broke under Xcode 27; all are fixed.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, site icons, a fire button and translation. Mac is in review, iPhone waits.

## Nov 9

The iPhone and Mac update is in review; the web is live. Joshua Tree's next build merged.

## Nov 10

Hamurapi's trailer is approved everywhere.

Apple rejected Patois for not signing in with Apple, which was never spam. Fixed; the iPhone build is back in review.

## Nov 11

Typed math answers count in any order, and math courses show x², √2 and π. Pre-Calc 11 went from ten to sixty questions; Pre-Calc 12 explains every answer.

## Nov 13

Apple locked my developer account until I accepted a new agreement. Tocks was rejected for background audio, and Apple asked what Delete account does; lost-pet posts go too. Co-Stanza is live on iPhone and Mac.

## Nov 14

Sent an iPhone update to Apple. The Nimble name belongs to another developer.

## Nov 15

Joshua Tree's dimming and saver are merged. The Pi's auto lock is in review: only my password opens it.

I built the three pieces Doorstock's spec was missing: spring costs, a label scanner and a monthly accountant CSV. Mac is built; iPhone waits on the appeal. Talli got a money log with a yearly limit bar; web and server are live.

Kernel imports a PDF or EPUB you own on every device and summarizes each chapter. The library then came up empty; I recovered most summaries from cache.

Epiphany is free on the web; Pro is $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year, and anyone who paid $1 keeps it for life. I shut three billing holes; the iPhone paywall is next. I killed Gato and the CanLII app, since Temida does that search.

## Nov 16

Board night ran past midnight on a stale card with the wrong clock, which broke every secure download. Paddle froze because every frame repainted the whole screen; now it repaints only what moved. I started a carrier board for the Compute Module 5.

Joshua Tree's version 14 was meant for printing, but with no printer it shipped as graphics acceleration; the Mac now drives it. I started a 64-bit PC port. Samantha's captioner writes captions by listening; the first test was word for word. Her trailer is done. The LaCie is gone, taking the 9B model's data with it.

All 58 Apple Notes went into 26 projects' roadmaps, private bits into my vault; Notes is empty.

## Nov 17

Keyrate got a new icon and a drill mode built from the keys I miss most; both are in review.

Samantha's video tools are finished: compress, grab a still, add a song. The landing opens on her trailer; scrolling no longer kills the live iPhone demo.

## Nov 18

The landing lost its typewriter headline. A new desktop section shows Spaces, Mission Control, Spotlight and Launchpad.

I graded the Joshua Tree landing a B-, cut its repeats and made the desktop clips one swipeable strip. Turing's picker reached its major release. A fresh sealed set of 420 rows passed all three bars; a new guard refuses about half as many right answers. It is on Hugging Face; both sealed sets are spent.

I freed 2.5 GB and fixed my Claude skills after a Codex find-and-replace mangled them.

Vancouver Vice's Unreal project lived only on the LaCie, so the city and builds are gone. I'm rebuilding from the web game and map data on GitHub.

## Nov 21

Airwell passed App Review and went live. Nimble's landing types a question and lands the answer huge. I fixed two API bugs: cancels errored and failures got cached.

Joshua Tree has 202 open issues, aiming for under 200. Filters ruined the desert wallpaper, so 0.25 credits bought a real wax-crayon painting. Joshua graded it A; still open: a true 16:9 cut, a quiet dock and a 16-plus colour kernel format.

A security audit of every server app found four real holes. Epiphany's CDN cached one person's private data by URL, so anyone could read it for an hour; fixed. Password reset links could open a custom app link, so one tap could take over an account; now only https pages open. Talli let any signed-in user read others' files, and Melete could be tricked into linking a squatted signup. All four shipped. Turing's public voice endpoint still lets anyone make his cloned voice say any text; locking it waits on me.

Apple said Epiphany's iPhone build had ads; it has none, so I resubmitted. Conics bounced on a name mismatch; fixed. Sighting's notes now cover account deletion, and it's back in. Mailbag's Gmail sign-in now takes an app password. Scrounger was rejected for user posts, so it got a terms screen with Report and Block; Apple may want a recording. Elah was rejected twice for spam; I resubmitted with a new map and icon.

I read 43 saved X posts in full, filed them in my vault and cleared them off X. They graded B, strong on tools and thin on original ideas, and now seed Melete's daily idea.

## Nov 22

The Connect Mouse shortcut froze for fifteen seconds at a time, so I rebuilt it as Tether, a menu bar app that revives a Bluetooth mouse. A review found ten problems; I fixed eight, and the brand went blue. Icelus and a few others earn money. Hormuz got the Keyrate look with ship and oil charts; the strait is still shut on day 268.

## Nov 23

I published Echo's new release: Word export everywhere and a Mac hotkey for dictation. I rebuilt Echo's landing around its live view, writing on a desk. Patois got its parrot icon back and fresh screenshots. I cut Vancouver Vice's stale screenshots. Tocks swapped its clock tiles for one huge live clock. Duke's landing opens on the app, nine games drifting past, then the live Pokemon graph. Talli's landing leads with "See your next cheque" and shows the real app. Hamurapi's landing shows the game with an App Store link. I redrew the 32 Things icons as realistic metal, glass and wood. Each is its own file, and they're live.

A pull wiped my terminal settings yesterday. I restored them from GitHub and stopped tracking them so it can't happen again. Two skills are still out.
