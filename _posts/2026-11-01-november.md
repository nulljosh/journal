## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check and twenty apps got fixes. Sighting and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Joshua Tree first ran full screen on a real Pi 4; board testing found five bugs, all fixed.

## Nov 2

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube; its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes, added eleven Kernel chapters from four books, and deleted 184 source photos; 566 wait.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam over two typing tests; I rebuilt its word list from public-domain novels; both are back in review.

## Nov 7

Nine projects broke under Xcode 27; all are fixed.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, site icons, a fire button and translation. Mac is in review, iPhone waits.

## Nov 9

## Nov 10

Apple rejected Patois for not signing in with Apple, which was never spam. Fixed; the iPhone build is back in review.

## Nov 11

Typed math answers count in any order, and math courses show x², √2 and π. Pre-Calc 11 went from ten to sixty questions, and 12 explains every answer.

## Nov 13

Apple locked my account until I accepted a new agreement. Tocks was rejected for background audio, and Apple asked what Delete account does; lost-pet posts go too. Co-Stanza is live on iPhone and Mac.

## Nov 14

Sent an iPhone update to Apple. The Nimble name is taken by another developer.

## Nov 15

The Pi's auto lock is in review: only my password opens it.

I built the three pieces Doorstock's spec lacked: spring costs, a label scanner and a monthly accountant CSV. Mac is built; iPhone waits on appeal. Talli got a money log with a yearly limit bar, live on web and server.

Kernel imports a book you own and summarizes each chapter. Epiphany is free on the web; Pro is $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year, and anyone who paid $1 keeps it for life. I shut three billing holes; the iPhone paywall is next. I killed Gato and the CanLII app; Temida does that search.

## Nov 16

Board night ran past midnight; a stale card's clock broke secure downloads. Paddle froze because every frame repainted everything; now only what moved repaints. I started a carrier board for the Compute Module 5.

Joshua Tree shipped graphics acceleration; the Mac now drives it. I started a 64-bit PC port. Samantha's captioner writes captions by listening; the first test was word for word. Her trailer is done.
## Nov 17

Keyrate got a new icon and a drill mode built from the keys I miss most.

Samantha's video tools are done.

## Nov 18

A new desktop section shows Spaces, Mission Control, Spotlight and Launchpad.

I graded the Joshua Tree landing a B-, cut its repeats and made the desktop clips one swipe strip. Turing's picker reached its major release. A fresh sealed set of 420 rows passed all three bars; a new guard refuses about half as many right answers. Both sets are spent.

Vancouver Vice's Unreal project lived only on the LaCie, so the city and builds are gone; rebuilding from the web game and map data.

## Nov 21

Airwell passed App Review and went live. I fixed two API bugs: cancels errored and failures got cached.

Filters ruined the desert wallpaper, so a 0.25-credit wax-crayon painting replaced it. I graded it A; open: a true 16:9 cut and a quiet dock.

A security audit of every server app found four real holes, now shipped. Epiphany's CDN cached one person's private data by URL for an hour; password reset links could open any app link, so one tap could take over an account; Talli let signed-in users read others' files; Melete could link a squatted signup. Turing's public voice endpoint still lets anyone make his cloned voice say any text; locking it waits on me.

Apple said Epiphany's iPhone build had ads; it has none, so I resubmitted. Scrounger was rejected for user posts; it got a terms screen with Report and Block. Elah was rejected twice for spam; I resubmitted with a new map and icon.

I cleared 43 saved X posts; they graded B, strong on tools, thin on ideas, and now seed Melete's daily idea.

## Nov 22

The Connect Mouse shortcut froze for fifteen seconds at a time, so I rebuilt it as Tether, a menu bar app that revives a mouse. A review found ten problems; I fixed eight. Hormuz got the Keyrate look; the strait is still shut, day 268.

## Nov 23

I shipped Echo's release with Word export and a Mac dictation hotkey, and rebuilt its landing around the live view. Patois got its parrot icon back, and I redrew the 32 Things icons as metal, glass and wood. I asked Apple for its bigger cloud model; Nimble, Echo, Cyllene and Plaintxt get it once it lands.

I sorted 87 Apple Notes into 25 roadmaps, then emptied Notes. My sticker proof is approved and in production.

Kernel got more books: Hackers and Painters is whole, Claude AI for Dummies has chapters 4 to 16, Video Game Design for Dummies has 13 and 14, and Einstein and Relativity is new. I rewrote the guide in No Starch style: a big chapter number, a Bruegel painting and a Try It Yourself box per chapter.

Scrounger's grey photos and wrong city names are fixed.

## Nov 25

I checked the App Store numbers, saw almost nobody buying, and made nine small apps free as an experiment. Echo, Kernel and Epiphany stay as they are. On December 23 I'll check downloads to see whether price or discovery was the problem.

Airwell got the biggest push: themes, a welcome screen and eleven languages, checked in German and Arabic at the largest text size. The update is with Apple; the Product Hunt launch waits.

I cleaned up App Store Connect. I deleted Oohaah and Sidewise, so the account is down from 34 apps to 32, and dropped China from all 21 live apps to keep Apple's new China AI and security banners off them. I wrote a skill that sweeps the popups and blockers until a clean pass. Apple's reasons are in: Elah and Cartons are spam and need real changes, Mailbag and Scrounger got 2.1 issues on an iPad Air review, Sighting got a 2.1 question about account deletion, and Plaintxt's Mac version is approved.

Epiphany's copy had fallen weeks behind GitHub, so I reapplied only the lost fixes. Business News now alternates sources. The US Get Pro button, linking to Stripe, is with Apple; once approved, the app goes free and Pro is sold on the web.

I spent the rest of the day on bigger text and screen readers. Text in seventeen apps grows with the text size setting, and icon-only buttons speak their labels. I wrote an iOS guide and checker, and Machaon's lab charts got a spoken summary. I'm back at KPU in January; the aid paperwork is done.
