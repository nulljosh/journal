## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check, and twenty apps got fixes. Sighting and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Joshua Tree ran full screen on a real Raspberry Pi 4 for the first time. Board testing found five bugs, all fixed that afternoon; release eight passed.

## Nov 2

Duke ran Papers, Please overnight; it reached day 14 and learned to defuse the bomb.

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube; its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote by hand. Keyrate got its App Store record, free and collecting nothing.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes, added eleven Kernel chapters from four books, and deleted 184 source photos; 566 wait.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam, matching its word list to two typing tests. I rebuilt the list from public domain novels; both are back in review.

## Nov 7

Cyllene is back before Apple. Nine projects broke under Xcode 27; all are fixed.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes.
After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, site icons, a fire button and translation. Mac is in review, iPhone waits.

## Nov 8

Plaintxt's builds wait; Apple wants bigger iOS changes or I drop iOS.
## Nov 9

The iPhone and Mac update is in review; the web is live. Joshua Tree's next build merged, closing 32 issues.
## Nov 10

Hamurapi's trailer is approved everywhere.

Apple rejected Patois for not signing in with Apple, which was never spam. The sheet stayed open after sign-in, so it looked dead. Fixed; the iPhone build is back in review.

## Nov 11

Typed math answers count in any order, and math courses show x², √2 and π. Pre-Calc 11 went from ten to sixty questions; Pre-Calc 12 explains every answer.

I fixed Patois still saying Tonchi in reset emails.
## Nov 13

Apple locked my developer account until I accepted a new agreement. Tocks was rejected for background audio. Apple asked what Delete account does; lost-pet posts now go too. Co-Stanza is live on iPhone and Mac.

## Nov 14

Sent an iPhone update to Apple. The one-word Nimble name belongs to another developer.

## Nov 15

Joshua Tree's dimming and saver are merged. The Pi's auto lock is in review: only my password opens it.

I built the three pieces Doorstock's spec was missing: spring costs, a label scanner and a monthly accountant CSV. Mac is built; iPhone waits on the appeal.

Talli got a money log with a yearly limit bar; web and server are live.

Kernel now imports a PDF or EPUB you own on every device and summarizes each chapter. Then the library came up empty; I recovered most summaries from a cache.

Epiphany is free on the web now; Pro is $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year, and anyone who paid $1 keeps it for life. I shut three billing holes; the iPhone paywall is next. I killed Gato and dropped the CanLII app; Temida does that search.

## Nov 16

Board night ran past midnight on a stale card with the wrong clock, so every secure download failed. Paddle froze because every frame repainted the whole screen; now it paints only what moved. I also started a carrier board for the Compute Module 5.

Joshua Tree's version 14 was meant for printing, but I have no printer, so it shipped as graphics acceleration. The Mac now drives the Pi and sees its screen. I also started the 64-bit PC port, which boots in the emulator.
Samantha writes captions by listening; the first test came out word for word. Her trailer is done: thirty seconds, narrated. Her training runs alone now, with safer precision after a math overflow. The LaCie is gone, taking the 9B model's data with it.

All 58 Apple Notes went into the roadmaps of 26 projects, private bits into my vault; Notes is empty.

## Nov 17

Keyrate got a new icon and a drill mode built from the keys I miss most. Both Keyrate builds still wait for review.

Samantha's video tools are finished: compress a clip, grab a still, lay a song under a video. The landing opens on her trailer behind the headline, and scrolling no longer kills the live iPhone demo.

The 4B still can't debug a config or run auto mode; the 9B needs 15 GB I don't have.

## Nov 18

The landing lost its typewriter headline for good; a build check now blocks it. A new desktop section shows Spaces, Mission Control, Spotlight and Launchpad. Next is the Pi drawing its desktop, once Joshua tests it.
I graded the Joshua Tree landing a B-: good content, muddled order, too many repeats. I cut the repeats and made the desktop clips one swipeable strip.Turing's picker reached its major release. A fresh sealed set of 420 rows passed all three bars, and a new guard refuses about half as many right answers. It is on Hugging Face; both sealed sets are spent.

I tidied the code folder, freeing 2.5 GB, and repaired my Claude skills after a Codex find-and-replace mangled them; all 29 now link to one shared folder.

Vancouver Vice's Unreal project lived only on the LaCie, so the city and builds are gone. The web game and map data survived on GitHub; I'm rebuilding from those.

## Nov 21

Airwell passed App Review and went live on the App Store.

Nimble's landing now types a question and lands the answer huge behind the headline. I fixed two API bugs: cancels errored, and failures were cached.

I ran a security audit across every app with a server and found four real holes. Epiphany was letting its CDN cache one person's private data by URL, so anyone could read it for an hour; that is fixed, and GitHub sign-in no longer trusts an unverified email. Password reset links in jaybulb-api could open a custom app link, so one tap could take over any account; now they only open our https pages. Talli let any signed-in user read other people's files, and Melete could be tricked into linking a squatted signup. All four are shipped. Turing's public voice endpoint still lets anyone make his cloned voice say any text; locking it to Turing's own words is waiting on my call.

Apple's automated check said Epiphany's iPhone build had ads; it has none, so I said so and resubmitted. Conics was bounced because the build's name didn't match, and the new build says Conics. Sighting's review notes now cover account deletion, and it went back in. Mailbag's Gmail sign-in got stuck on Google's unverified-app page, so on iPhone and iPad it takes a Google app password, the way iCloud does. Scrounger was rejected for user posts, so it got a terms screen, Report and Block; Apple may want a screen recording on a real phone, and that part is on me. Elah was rejected twice for spam, so I sent a version with a cross-reference map and a new icon.
