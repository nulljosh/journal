## Nov 1

Accessibility took the day: 98 pages pass an automated check and twenty apps got fixes. Sighting and Co-Stanza went to App Review; Apple wants every post reportable and blockable. Joshua Tree first ran full screen on a Pi 4; board testing found five bugs, fixed.

## Nov 2

Joshua Tree shipped Shortcuts, Bluetooth and YouTube; its Pi graphics chip runs on a driver I wrote.

## Nov 6

I filed 31 Apple Notes, added eleven Kernel chapters from four books, and deleted 184 source photos; 566 wait.

Apple rejected Keyrate as spam over two typing tests; I rebuilt its word list from public-domain novels, and it's back in review.

## Nov 7

Nine projects broke under Xcode 27 and are fixed.

Apple rejected Elah, Co-Stanza and Sighting as spam lookalikes. After comparing ten rival browsers, I added downloads, site icons, a fire button and translation. Mac is in review, iPhone waits.

## Nov 10

Apple rejected Patois for not signing in with Apple, which was never spam. Fixed; the iPhone build is back in review.

## Nov 11

Typed math answers count in any order, and math courses show x², √2 and π.

## Nov 13

Apple locked my account until I accepted a new agreement. Tocks was rejected for background audio, and Apple asked what Delete account does; lost-pet posts go too. Co-Stanza is live on iPhone and Mac.

## Nov 14

Sent an iPhone update, but another developer has the Nimble name.

## Nov 15

The Pi's auto lock is in review: only my password opens it.

I built the three pieces Doorstock's spec lacked: spring costs, a label scanner and an accountant CSV. Mac is built; iPhone waits on appeal. Talli got a money log with a yearly limit bar, live on web and server.

Epiphany is free on the web; Pro is $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year, and $1 buyers keep it for life. I shut three billing holes; the iPhone paywall is next. I killed Gato and the CanLII app; Temida does that search.

## Nov 16

A stale card clock broke secure downloads. Paddle froze from repainting every frame; now only moving parts repaint. I started a carrier board for the Compute Module 5.

Joshua Tree shipped graphics acceleration; the Mac now drives it. I started a 64-bit PC port. Samantha's captioner wrote captions by listening, word for word.

## Nov 18

A new desktop section shows Spaces, Mission Control, Spotlight and Launchpad.

I graded the Joshua Tree landing a B-, cut its repeats and made the desktop clips one swipe strip. Turing's picker hit its major release.

Vancouver Vice's Unreal project was lost with the LaCie; I'm rebuilding it from the web game's data.

## Nov 21

Airwell passed App Review and went live. I fixed two API bugs: cancels errored and failures got cached.

Filters ruined the desert wallpaper, so a wax-crayon painting replaced it.

A security audit of every server app found four real holes, all shipped. Epiphany's CDN cached one person's private data by URL for an hour; reset links could open any app link, so one tap could take over an account; Talli let users read others' files; Melete could link a squatted signup. Turing's public voice endpoint still lets anyone make his cloned voice say any text; locking it is mine.

Apple said Epiphany's iPhone build had ads; it has none, so I resubmitted. Scrounger was rejected for user posts; it got a terms screen with Report and Block. Elah was rejected twice for spam; I resubmitted with a new map and icon.

I cleared 43 saved X posts; they graded B, strong on tools, thin on ideas, and seed Melete's idea.

## Nov 22

The Connect Mouse shortcut froze for fifteen seconds, so I rebuilt it as Tether, a menu bar app that revives a mouse. A review found ten problems; I fixed eight. Hormuz got the Keyrate look; the strait is still shut, day 268.

## Nov 23

I shipped Echo's release with Word export and a Mac dictation hotkey, and rebuilt its landing around the live view. Patois got its parrot icon back, and I redrew the 32 Things icons. I asked Apple for its bigger cloud model; four apps get it once it lands.

I sorted 87 Apple Notes into 25 roadmaps and emptied Notes. My sticker proof is approved and in production.

Kernel got four more books, including Einstein and Relativity.

Scrounger's grey photos and wrong city names are fixed.

## Nov 25

Almost nobody was buying, so nine small apps went free as a test, leaving Echo, Kernel and Epiphany alone. On December 23 I'll check whether price or discovery was the problem.

Airwell got the biggest push: themes, a welcome screen and eleven languages, checked in German and Arabic at the largest text size. The update is with Apple; its Product Hunt launch waits.

I cleaned up App Store Connect: deleted Oohaah and Sidewise, and dropped China from all 21 live apps to kill Apple's China banners. Apple's reasons: Elah and Cartons are spam, Mailbag and Scrounger got 2.1 issues.

Epiphany's copy had fallen weeks behind GitHub; I reapplied the lost fixes. Its Get Pro button is with Apple; once approved, the app goes free and sells Pro on the web.

The rest of the day went to bigger text and screen readers: seventeen apps grow with text size.

## Nov 26

My Bluetooth mouse dropped overnight, and Tether couldn't revive it because it came back under a new address. I re-paired it by hand, then taught Tether to do that itself after 15 seconds. Not yet tried on a real drop.

Our uptime check pinged a dead site every half hour, so I cut it. Blackcomb's welcome screen, fade-in and start page are waiting on Apple. I'm taking math at KPU in January.

Bugle's landing runs on a live feed, and its App Store copy leads with news.

My promo tool cuts YouTube walkthroughs and TikTok ads from real screens. Ten apps have videos; six wait on retakes for old names on screen.

Flamingo Map got its App Store listing and a live landing. Cyllene's reply drafter falls back to a local model, then Claude, splits Apple's numbered questions, and holds back answers only I can give. The Flamingo reply is drafted; the screen recording is on take three after two missed the tab bar. Nothing has gone to Apple; Cyllene's iPhone app is live.

Product Hunt took the rest of the day. I went through all 32 products, fixed old names, and put back a price a script had blanked. Fourteen apps without a launch got kits, Costanza ready to schedule. Nothing's scheduled.

The portfolio site had prices that didn't match the store, so I rebuilt its price column from App Store Connect. Seven apps it listed at 99 cents are actually free, and Epiphany is now 99 cents on the store to match Echo and Kernel. None of the three paid apps has in-app purchase yet, so I'm moving them to free with a 99 cent premium unlock, and the price flips to free only once that build is approved.
